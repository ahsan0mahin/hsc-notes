River–boat and current

Question 66

Notre Dame College, Mymensingh · Board question no. 1 · book p. 64

Description of the figure (Question 66 – stem):
Two parallel horizontal banks of a river, with BD=150 mBD = 150\text{ m} on the upper bank.
Point AA on the lower bank; on the upper bank, point BB directly opposite AA, and point DD to the right.
In the middle of the river, an arrow pointing right: velocity of the current = 6ms⁻¹.

In the figure, ABAB is the width of the river. Mr. Jamal's house is at point AA and his office is at point DD. On Wednesday morning, in order to reach the office within 55 minutes, Mr. Jamal started rowing a boat from point AA at a velocity of 10ms−110\text{ms}^{-1}, making an angle 60∘60^\circ with the direction opposite to the current, and reached the office.

Part c · 3 marks

How many metres did Mr. Jamal's boat travel to cross the river in the stem?

Hint Try it first

BD is the downstream drift, not the width. Use the along-bank component to obtain the time.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

vx=6+10cos⁡120∘,vy=10sin⁡120∘v_x=6+10\cos120^\circ,\quad v_y=10\sin120^\circ

Net downstream speed is 6−10 cos60°=1 m/s; across speed is 10 sin60°. Time=150/1=150 s, so the path is 150√76≈1308 m.

Part d · 4 marks

Explain mathematically whether Mr. Jamal in the stem can reach the office within 55 minutes.

Hint Try it first

Compare the time corresponding to that drift with five minutes in the same units.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t=150/vx=150 st=150/v_x=150\,\mathrm s

150 s=2.5 min, less than the five-minute allowance.
