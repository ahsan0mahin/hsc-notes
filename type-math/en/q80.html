River–boat and current

Question 80

Halishahar Cantonment Public School & College, Chattogram · Board question no. 2 · book p. 69

One day Rian went to swim in a river that has a current. The current of the river flows from west to east with velocity 2 km/h2\text{ km/h}. To go straight from the south bank to the north bank of the river, Rian started swimming with velocity 4 km/h4\text{ km/h} making an angle with the direction of the current.

Part c · 3 marks

In the light of the stem, determine Rian's direction of swimming.

Hint Try it first

Treat north and east as separate axes. Which component of swimming velocity should point west?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

4sin⁡θ=24\sin\theta=2

To cancel eastward drift he needs a 2 km/h westward component. Thus aim arcsin(2/4)=30° west of north.

Part d · 4 marks

Explain through mathematical analysis whether the times taken to cross the river along the shortest path and in the shortest time will be equal.

Hint Try it first

For a symbolic width d write times for maximum across-bank speed and for zero drift separately.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tdirect=d/12>d/4=tmin⁡t_{\rm direct}=d/\sqrt{12}>d/4=t_{\min}

For width d the direct-landing time is d/√12. The minimum time is d/4, achieved by heading due north and drifting east. The times differ.
