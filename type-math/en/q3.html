Three points P(4,−2,3)P(4, -2, 3), Q(−2,4,5)Q(-2, 4, 5) and R(−1,2,−2)R(-1, 2, -2) lie in a three-dimensional rectangular coordinate system.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the unit vector of PQ→×QR→\overrightarrow{PQ} \times \overrightarrow{QR}.

Hint Try it first

Form Q−P and R−Q before crossing them. What must you divide the result by?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

PQ→=(−2−4)i^+(4+2)j^+(5−3)k^\overrightarrow{PQ}=(-2-4)\hat i+(4+2)\hat j+(5-3)\hat k

PQ→=−6i^+6j^+2k^\overrightarrow{PQ} = -6\hat i+6\hat j+2\hat k

QR→=(−1+2)i^+(2−4)j^+(−2−5)k^\overrightarrow{QR}=(-1+2)\hat i+(2-4)\hat j+(-2-5)\hat k

QR→=i^−2j^−7k^.\overrightarrow{QR} = \hat i-2\hat j-7\hat k.
Here, the three points—P(4, −2, 3), Q(−2, 4, 5), R(−1, 2, −2).

Part d · 4 marks

Will the three points in the stem form a right-angled triangle? Analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Compare the largest squared side with the sum of the other two.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

From (c) we get,

PQ→=−6i^+6j^+2k^,\overrightarrow{PQ}=-6\hat i+6\hat j+2\hat k,
QR→=i^−2j^−7k^.\overrightarrow{QR}=\hat i-2\hat j-7\hat k.
Now,

PR→=(−1−4)i^+(2+2)j^+(−2−3)k^\overrightarrow{PR}=(-1-4)\hat i+(2+2)\hat j+(-2-3)\hat k

PR→=−5i^+4j^−k^\overrightarrow{PR} = -5\hat i+4\hat j-\hat k

∣PQ→∣=(−6)2+62+22|\overrightarrow{PQ}|=\sqrt{(-6)^2+6^2+2^2}

∣PQ→∣=219|\overrightarrow{PQ}| = 2\sqrt{19}
∣QR→∣=12+(−2)2+(−7)2|\overrightarrow{QR}|=\sqrt{1^2+(-2)^2+(-7)^2}

∣QR→∣=36|\overrightarrow{QR}| = 3\sqrt6
∣PR→∣=(−5)2+42+(−1)2|\overrightarrow{PR}|=\sqrt{(-5)^2+4^2+(-1)^2}

∣PR→∣=42.|\overrightarrow{PR}| = \sqrt{42}.
Here, three points: P(4, −2, 3), Q(−2, 4, 5), R(−1, 2, −2).
Now,

∣QR→∣2+∣PR→∣2=(36)2+(42)2=96|\overrightarrow{QR}|^2+|\overrightarrow{PR}|^2=(3\sqrt6)^2+(\sqrt{42})^2=96
and

∣PQ→∣2=(219)2|\overrightarrow{PQ}|^2=(2\sqrt{19})^2

∣PQ→∣2=76.|\overrightarrow{PQ}|^2 = 76.

∴∣PQ→∣2≠∣QR→∣2+∣PR→∣2.\therefore|\overrightarrow{PQ}|^2\ne|\overrightarrow{QR}|^2+|\overrightarrow{PR}|^2.
∴ the three points in the stem do not form a right-angled triangle.
In the z-component of the original's calculation of PR→\overrightarrow{PR}, (-2−3) is written but the result is printed as −1. Correct PR→=−5i^+4j^−5k^\overrightarrow{PR}=-5\hat i+4\hat j-5\hat k and ∣PR→∣=66|\overrightarrow{PR}|=\sqrt{66}. The squares of the three sides are 76, 54, 66; no two of them add up to the third. Hence the conclusion 'not right-angled' is correct, but the intermediate calculation is wrong. The original answer also shows only one inequality without checking the longest side.
