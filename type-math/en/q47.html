Question 47

Joypurhat Girls' Cadet College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 54

The position vectors of points AA, BB and CC are respectively:
rA=i^+2j^−k^rB=3i^−j^+2k^rC=2i^+5j^−4k^r_A = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - \hat{k} \qquad r_B = 3\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 2\hat{k} \qquad r_C = 2\hat{i} + 5\hat{j} - 4\hat{k}

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the angle between AB→\overrightarrow{AB} and AC→\overrightarrow{AC}.

Hint Try it first

Both arrows must begin at A. Find their dot product and their two lengths.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡θ=−162219\cos\theta=\frac{-16}{\sqrt{22}\sqrt{19}}

AB=(2,−3,3), AC=(1,3,−3). Their dot product is −16. Divide by √22 times √19 and take arccos: about 141.5°.

Part d · 4 marks

Are the three points AA, BB and CC collinear or coplanar? Verify mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Check if one displacement is a scalar multiple of the other.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(2,−3,3)×(1,3,−3)=(0,9,9)≠0⃗(2,-3,3)\times(1,3,-3)=(0,9,9)\ne\vec0

The two displacement vectors are not proportional, so the points are not collinear. Three noncollinear points determine a plane.
