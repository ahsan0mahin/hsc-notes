Cumilla Board · 2023 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 46

A scalar field A⃗=3i^+2j^+k^\vec{A} = 3\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + \hat{k}, a vector field B⃗=i^+2j^+3k^\vec{B} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 3\hat{k} and a vector operator C⃗=i^+2j^+2k^\vec{C} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}

[book printing error: in the book the operator is printed with \vec{V} and a partial-differentiation \delta sign]

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the perpendicular projection of A⃗\vec{A} along B⃗\vec{B}.

Hint Try it first

The reference direction is B; which length should divide the dot product?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

3+4+31+4+9=1014\frac{3+4+3}{\sqrt{1+4+9}}=\frac{10}{\sqrt{14}}

A·B=10 and the length of B is √14. The projection is 10/√14≈2.673.

Part d · 4 marks

Will the three vectors A⃗,B⃗\vec{A}, \vec{B} and C⃗\vec{C} lie in the same plane — analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Compute B×C and test whether A has a component along it.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(3,2,1)⋅(−2,1,0)=−4(3,2,1)\cdot(-2,1,0)=-4

B×C=(−2,1,0); dotting this with A gives −4. Nonzero triple product rules out coplanarity.
