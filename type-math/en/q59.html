Part d · 4 marks

Can a rectangular solid be made from the three vectors in the stem? Analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

One nonperpendicular pair is enough to exclude a rectangular solid.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A rectangular solid can be made from three vectors only when the three vectors are mutually perpendicular. If the three vectors are mutually perpendicular, the dot product of any two of them will be zero.
Here,

A⃗=2i^+j^+3k^,B⃗\vec A=2\hat i+\hat j+3\hat k,\quad\vec B

A⃗=i^+2j^−k^,C⃗\vec A = \hat i+2\hat j-\hat k,\quad\vec C

A⃗=i^+3j^+2k^.\vec A = \hat i+3\hat j+2\hat k.

A⃗⋅B⃗=(2)(1)+(1)(2)+(3)(−1)\vec A\cdot\vec B=(2)(1)+(1)(2)+(3)(-1)
or, A⃗⋅B⃗=2+2−3\vec A\cdot\vec B=2+2-3

∴A⃗⋅B⃗=1.\therefore\vec A\cdot\vec B=1.
since A⃗⋅B⃗≠0\vec A\cdot\vec B\ne0, therefore A⃗\vec A and B⃗\vec B are not mutually perpendicular.

B⃗⋅C⃗=(1)(1)+(2)(3)+(−1)(2)\vec B\cdot\vec C=(1)(1)+(2)(3)+(-1)(2)
or, B⃗⋅C⃗=1+6−2\vec B\cdot\vec C=1+6-2

∴B⃗⋅C⃗=5.\therefore\vec B\cdot\vec C=5.
since B⃗⋅C⃗≠0\vec B\cdot\vec C\ne0, therefore B⃗\vec B and C⃗\vec C are not mutually perpendicular.

A⃗⋅C⃗=(2)(1)+(1)(3)+(3)(2)\vec A\cdot\vec C=(2)(1)+(1)(3)+(3)(2)
or, A⃗⋅C⃗=2+3+6\vec A\cdot\vec C=2+3+6

∴A⃗⋅C⃗=11.\therefore\vec A\cdot\vec C=11.
since A⃗⋅C⃗≠0\vec A\cdot\vec C\ne0, therefore A⃗\vec A and C⃗\vec C are not mutually perpendicular.
Since no pair of the three vectors is mutually perpendicular, therefore a rectangular solid cannot be made from the three vectors of the stem.
