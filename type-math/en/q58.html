River–boat and current

Question 58

Milestone College, Dhaka · Board question no. 1 · book p. 60

Boatman Kuber fishes in the river; he sets off from point OO in a boat along OAOA with velocity 12 kmh−112\text{ kmh}^{-1}. After 1010 minutes he hears on Bangladesh Betar that he must reach the bank at a safe place within 3030 minutes. The boatman immediately hoists the sail, so that 40%40\% of the wind's velocity is added to the boat's velocity along OAOA and the boat speeds up. The velocity of the wind along OAOA is 20kmh−120\text{kmh}^{-1}, and before the sail was hoisted the effect of the wind on the boat was negligible.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine at what angle the boat would have reached the other bank if the sail had not been hoisted.

Hint Try it first

Before raising the sail, combine the current with the perpendicular rowing speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

If the resultant velocity vv makes an angle θ\theta with the direction of the river's width (OAOA), then
or, tan⁡θ=vcvb\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{v_c}{v_b}
or, tan⁡θ=4 kmh−112 kmh−1\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{4\text{ kmh}^{-1}}{12\text{ kmh}^{-1}}

or, tan⁡θ=13\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{1}{3}
or, θ=tan⁡−1(13)\text{or, } \theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)

or, θ=18.4349∘ Ans.\text{or, } \theta = 18.4349^\circ\text{ Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

Will the boatman be able to reach the safe place in time? Give a mathematical explanation.

Hint Try it first

Split the trip at the radio warning. Apply the wind contribution only to the remaining width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

First step (before hoisting the sail): Distance covered along the width during this time,
dy1=vy1×t1d_{y1} = v_{y1} \times t_1

dy1=12 kmh−1×16 hourd_{y1} = 12\text{ kmh}^{-1} \times \frac{1}{6}\text{ hour}

dy1=2 kmd_{y1} = 2\text{ km}

Second step, Because of hoisting the sail, the new velocity along the width of the river becomes:
vy2=vy1+Δvbv_{y2} = v_{y1} + \Delta v_b

vy2=12 kmh−1+8 kmh−1v_{y2} = 12\text{ kmh}^{-1} + 8\text{ kmh}^{-1}

vy2=20 kmh−1v_{y2} = 20\text{ kmh}^{-1} Time required to cover the remaining distance D2D_2 (t2t_2):
t2=D2vy2t_2 = \frac{D_2}{v_{y2}}

t2=8 km20 kmh−1t_2 = \frac{8\text{ km}}{20\text{ kmh}^{-1}}

t2=0.4 hourt_2 = 0.4\text{ hour}
Total time for the boat to reach the other bank, (t1+t2)=16 hour+0.4 hour=0.5667 hour(t_1 + t_2) = \frac{1}{6}\text{ hour} + 0.4\text{ hour} = 0.5667\text{ hour} The boat takes a total time of 0.567 hour0.567\text{ hour} (about 3434 minutes) to reach the other bank. The total time limit for reaching the safe place was Ttotal=40T_{\text{total}} = 40 minutes. Since the boat takes 3434 minutes to arrive, which is less than the stipulated 4040 minutes, therefore the boatman will be able to reach the other bank on time.
