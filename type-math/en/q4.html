River–boat and current

Question 4

Cumilla Board · 2025 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 31

The velocity of an engine-powered boat with the current is 15 kmh−115\text{ kmh}^{-1} and against the current is 5 kmh−15\text{ kmh}^{-1}. In a river of width 5 km5\text{ km}, a boatman starts from point AA in order to reach point BB straight across on the other bank. From point CC, at a distance 5 km5\text{ km} from point BB, another person starts moving at the same time with a velocity of 4 kmh−14\text{ kmh}^{-1} towards point BB.

Part c · 3 marks

At what angle had the boatman set off in order to reach point BB?

Part d · 4 marks

Analyse mathematically whether the boatman met the other person when he reached point BB.
