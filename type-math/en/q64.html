Question 64

Abdul Kadir Mollah City College, Narsingdi · Board question no. 6 · book p. 63

A⃗=2i^+2j^−k^\vec{A} = 2\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - \hat{k}
B⃗=6i^−3j^+2k^\vec{B} = 6\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}
The two vectors represent two sides of a parallelogram.

[printed as \bar{A}, \bar{B} in the book]

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the perpendicular projection of B⃗\vec{B} on A⃗\vec{A} between the two vectors.

Hint Try it first

A is the reference direction. Normalize it before taking its dot product with B.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

compAB=12−6−23\mathrm{comp}_A B=\frac{12-6-2}{3}

A·B=12−6−2=4 and A has length 3. The projection is 4/3.

Part d · 4 marks

Give your opinion, through mathematical analysis, on whether A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B} can be adjacent sides of a parallelogram.

Hint Try it first

Adjacent sides need not be perpendicular, but they must span a nonzero area.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For two vectors to be adjacent sides of a parallelogram, the two vectors must be non-zero and non-collinear. Determining the magnitudes of the two vectors
∣A⃗∣=22+22+(−1)2|\vec{A}| = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2 + (-1)^2}

∣A⃗∣=3|\vec{A}| = 3
∣B⃗∣=62+(−3)2+22|\vec{B}| = \sqrt{6^2 + (-3)^2 + 2^2}

∣B⃗∣=36+9+4|\vec{B}| = \sqrt{36 + 9 + 4}

∣B⃗∣=49|\vec{B}| = \sqrt{49}

∣B⃗∣=7|\vec{B}| = 7
or, given,
A⃗=2i^+2j^−k^\vec{A} = 2\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - \hat{k}
B⃗=6i^−3j^+2k^\vec{B} = 6\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}
Since ∣A⃗∣≠0|\vec{A}| \neq 0 and ∣B⃗∣≠0|\vec{B}| \neq 0, therefore the two vectors are non-zero. If the two vectors are collinear, then A⃗=kB⃗\vec{A} = k\vec{B}, where kk is a scalar quantity. The two vectors will be collinear if the ratios of the components of A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B} are equal. Now, the ratio of the i^\hat{i} component: 26=13\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}, ratio of the j^\hat{j} component: 2−3=−23\frac{2}{-3} = -\frac{2}{3}, ratio of the k^\hat{k} component: −12=−12\frac{-1}{2} = -\frac{1}{2} From 26≠2−3≠−12\frac{2}{6} \neq \frac{2}{-3} \neq \frac{-1}{2} we get, Therefore, the vectors A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B} are not collinear. Since the two vectors A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B} are non-zero and non-collinear, they can represent two adjacent sides of a parallelogram. Therefore, a parallelogram can be formed with the two vectors A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B}.
