Rain and umbrella

Question 85

Barishal Government Women's College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 72

One day Shanta stood watching the rain fall. She observed that the rain is falling from the south at 8 ms−18\text{ ms}^{-1} at an angle of 40∘40^\circ with the vertical. Afterwards she started walking in the rain from south to north and, to protect herself from the rain, held the umbrella at an angle of 20∘20^\circ with the vertical towards her back.

Part c · 3 marks

What is the actual velocity of the rain?

Hint Try it first

Which reference frame does a stationary observer share?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Given, when Shanta was standing, she saw the rain falling at a velocity of 8 ms−18\text{ ms}^{-1} at an angle 40∘40^\circ with the vertical. Since Shanta was stationary, the velocity of the rain relative to her is the actual velocity of the rain.
∴\therefore the actual velocity of the rain, vr=8 ms−1 Ans.v_r = 8\text{ ms}^{-1}\text{ Ans.}

Verify mathematically whether the statement “Shanta's walking velocity is less than the actual velocity of the rain” is correct from the point of view of the stem.

Hint Try it first
