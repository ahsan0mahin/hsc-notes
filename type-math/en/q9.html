Gradient–divergence–curl

Question 9

Jashore Board · 2025 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 34

A vector quantity A⃗=4x3y2i^−4x2y3zj^−6xyz3k^\vec{A} = 4x^3y^2\hat{i} - 4x^2y^3z\hat{j} - 6xyz^3\hat{k} and a scalar quantity ϕ=3xy2z+3xyz2+3y2z3\phi = 3xy^2z + 3xyz^2 + 3y^2z^3.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the curl of the vector A⃗\vec{A}.

Hint Try it first

Write the components in their original order and take the cyclic differences of partial derivatives.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

given,
A⃗=4x3y2i^−4x2y3zj^−6xyz3k^\vec{A} = 4x^3y^2\hat{i} - 4x^2y^3z\hat{j} - 6xyz^3\hat{k} the curl of the vector A⃗\vec{A} = ∇⃗×A⃗\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{A}
<div class="math">∣i^j^k^δδxδδyδδz4x3y2−4x2y3z−6xyz3∣\left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \frac{\delta}{\delta x} & \frac{\delta}{\delta y} & \frac{\delta}{\delta z} \\ 4x^3y^2 & -4x^2y^3z & -6xyz^3 \end{matrix}\right|</div>
<div class="math">i^{δδy(−6xyz3)−δδz(−4x2y3z)}−j^{δδx(−6xyz3)−δδz(4x3y2)}+k^{δδx(−4x2y3z)−δδy(4x3y2)}\hat{i}\left\{\frac{\delta}{\delta y}(-6xyz^3) - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}(-4x^2y^3z)\right\} - \hat{j}\left\{\frac{\delta}{\delta x}(-6xyz^3) - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}(4x^3y^2)\right\} + \hat{k}\left\{\frac{\delta}{\delta x}(-4x^2y^3z) - \frac{\delta}{\delta y}(4x^3y^2)\right\}</div>
i^(−6xz3+4x2y3)−j^(−6yz3)+k^(−8xy3z−8x3y)\hat{i}(-6xz^3 + 4x^2y^3) - \hat{j}(-6yz^3) + \hat{k}(-8xy^3z - 8x^3y)
(4x2y3−6xz3)i^+6yz3j^−(8xy3z+8x3y)k^ Ans.(4x^2y^3 - 6xz^3)\hat{i} + 6yz^3\hat{j} - (8xy^3z + 8x^3y)\hat{k}\text{ Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

In the light of the stem, show that a scalar field can be converted into a vector field and a vector field into a scalar field. — Analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Start with φ for one transformation and A for the other. What should the output type be in each case?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

given,
A⃗=4x3y2i^−4x2y3zj^−6xyz3k^\vec{A} = 4x^3y^2\hat{i} - 4x^2y^3z\hat{j} - 6xyz^3\hat{k} is a vector field and ϕ=3xy2z+3xyz2−3y2z3\phi = 3xy^2z + 3xyz^2 - 3y^2z^3 now, grad ϕ=∇⃗ϕ\text{grad}\,\phi = \vec{\nabla}\phi
(i^δδx+j^δδy+k^δδz)(3xy2z+3xyz2+3y2z3)\left(\hat{i}\frac{\delta}{\delta x} + \hat{j}\frac{\delta}{\delta y} + \hat{k}\frac{\delta}{\delta z}\right)(3xy^2z + 3xyz^2 + 3y^2z^3)
i^(3y2z+3yz2)+j^(6xyz+3xz2+6yz3)+k^(3xy2+6xyz+9y2z2)\hat{i}(3y^2z + 3yz^2) + \hat{j}(6xyz + 3xz^2 + 6yz^3) + \hat{k}(3xy^2 + 6xyz + 9y^2z^2) Here grad ϕ\text{grad}\,\phi is a vector field,
∴\therefore a scalar field can be converted into a vector field. Since div A⃗=∇⃗⋅A⃗\text{div}\,\vec{A} = \vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{A}
<div class="math">(i^δδx+j^δδy+k^δδz)⋅(4x3y2i^−4x2y3zj^−6xyz3k^)\left(\hat{i}\frac{\delta}{\delta x} + \hat{j}\frac{\delta}{\delta y} + \hat{k}\frac{\delta}{\delta z}\right) \cdot \left(4x^3y^2\hat{i} - 4x^2y^3z\hat{j} - 6xyz^3\hat{k}\right)</div>
δδx(4x3y2)+δδy(−4x2y3z)+δδz(−6xyz3)\frac{\delta}{\delta x}(4x^3y^2) + \frac{\delta}{\delta y}(-4x^2y^3z) + \frac{\delta}{\delta z}(-6xyz^3)
12x2y2−12x2y2z−18xyz212x^2y^2 - 12x^2y^2z - 18xyz^2 Here div A⃗\text{div}\,\vec{A} is a scalar field.
∴\therefore a vector field can be converted into a scalar field. That is, a scalar field can be converted into a vector field through the gradient, and a vector field into a scalar field through the divergence.
