Safiuddin Sarker Academy & College, Gazipur · Board question no. 3 · book p. 63

The velocity of the boat with the current is 18kmh−118\text{kmh}^{-1} and against the current is 8kmh−18\text{kmh}^{-1}. The width of the river is 3 km3\text{ km}. The boat is rowed to reach some point straight across on the other bank. Another boat of the same speed is rowed at an angle 100∘100^\circ with the direction opposite to the current.

Part c · 3 marks

In which direction must it be rowed to reach exactly the opposite bank?

Hint Try it first

Add and subtract the measured velocities, then find the heading that cancels the current.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u=(18+8)/2,c=(18−8)/2u=(18+8)/2,\quad c=(18-8)/2

Boat speed is (18+8)/2=13 km/h; current speed is 5 km/h. Hence α=arccos(−5/13)≈112.62° to downstream.
