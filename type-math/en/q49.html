Rain and umbrella

Question 49

Rangpur Cadet College · Board question no. 7 · book p. 55

Saumik was standing by the window watching the rain. The vertical velocity of the rain was 6kmh−16\text{kmh}^{-1}. On the road Saumik saw one man walking with velocity 4kmh−14\text{kmh}^{-1} and another man riding a bicycle with velocity 8kmh−18\text{kmh}^{-1}. Their umbrellas were held at different angles.

Part c · 3 marks

At what angle did the man walking on the road hold his umbrella to save himself from the rain?

Hint Try it first

In the pedestrian’s frame the horizontal rain component has the opposite sign to walking velocity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know,
tan⁡θ=vmvr\tan\theta = \frac{v_m}{v_r}
or, tan⁡θ=46\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{4}{6}
or, θ=tan⁡−1(0.667)\text{or, } \theta = \tan^{-1}(0.667)
∴θ=33.69∘ Ans.\therefore \theta = 33.69^\circ\text{ Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

The two persons' umbrellas were not held at the same angle — verify the truth of Saumik's observation through mathematical explanation.

Hint Try it first

Repeat the component ratio with the cyclist’s speed, keeping the same vertical component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

In the case of the walking man, We know,
tan⁡θ1=v1vr\tan\theta_1 = \frac{v_1}{v_r}
or, tan⁡θ1=46\text{or, } \tan\theta_1 = \frac{4}{6}
or, θ1=tan⁡−1(0.667)\text{or, } \theta_1 = \tan^{-1}(0.667)
∴θ1=33.69∘\therefore \theta_1 = 33.69^\circ
and in the case of the cyclist,
tan⁡θ2=v2vr\tan\theta_2 = \frac{v_2}{v_r}
or, tan⁡θ2=86\text{or, } \tan\theta_2 = \frac{8}{6}
or, θ2=tan⁡−1(1.333)\text{or, } \theta_2 = \tan^{-1}(1.333)
∴θ2=53.13∘\therefore \theta_2 = 53.13^\circ Mathematical analysis shows that the angle with the vertical in the case of the walking man is ≠\neq and in the case of the cyclist... that is, both the walking man and the cyclist have to hold their umbrellas at different angles. Therefore Saumik's observation was correct.
