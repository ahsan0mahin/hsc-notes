Question 42

Dhaka Board · 2019 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 51

The driver of a car moving eastwards at 40 km40\text{ km} per hour saw a truck moving northwards at twice his speed per hour. The east direction is taken as the positive xx-axis and the north direction as the positive yy-axis.

Part c · 3 marks

In which direction was the truck actually moving?

Hint Try it first

The truck’s apparent velocity is measured relative to the driver. Add back the driver’s ground velocity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Let the truck have been moving towards the north-east with velocity vTv_T at an angle θ\theta with the north direction.

Part d · 4 marks

Analyse mathematically and show whether it is possible to determine a vector along the direction perpendicular to the plane in which the actual velocities of the truck and the car lie.

Hint Try it first

Apply a cross product to the ground velocities, not to the speed magnitudes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

In this case, taking the east direction as the XX-axis and the north direction as the YY-axis, The actual velocity of the car, from vc=40 km/hv_c = 40\text{ km/h} we get, Velocity vector of the car, v⃗c=40i^ km/h\vec{v}_c = 40\hat{i}\text{ km/h} Again, the actual velocity of the truck, vT=405 km/hv_T = 40\sqrt{5}\text{ km/h}, acting on the east side at an angle 26.57∘26.57^\circ with the north direction— Velocity vector of the truck, v⃗T=(405sin⁡26.57∘)i^+(405cos⁡26.57∘)j^=(40i^+80j^) km/h\vec{v}_T = (40\sqrt{5}\sin 26.57^\circ)\hat{i} + (40\sqrt{5}\cos 26.57^\circ)\hat{j} = (40\hat{i} + 80\hat{j})\text{ km/h} Now, v⃗c×v⃗T\vec{v}_c \times \vec{v}_T is a vector perpendicular to the plane of v⃗c\vec{v}_c and v⃗T\vec{v}_T,
∴v⃗c×v⃗T=∣i^j^k^400040800∣=i^×0−j^×0+k^(40×80−0)=3200k^\therefore \vec{v}_c \times \vec{v}_T = \left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 40 & 0 & 0 \\ 40 & 80 & 0 \end{matrix}\right| = \hat{i} \times 0 - \hat{j} \times 0 + \hat{k}(40 \times 80 - 0) = 3200\hat{k} Let the unit vector along the direction perpendicular to the plane in which v⃗c\vec{v}_c and v⃗T\vec{v}_T lie be = η^\hat{\eta}
∴η^=v⃗c×v⃗T∣v⃗c×v⃗T∣\therefore \hat{\eta} = \frac{\vec{v}_c \times \vec{v}_T}{|\vec{v}_c \times \vec{v}_T|}

η^=3200k^(3200)2\hat{\eta} = \frac{3200\hat{k}}{\sqrt{(3200)^2}}

η^=k^\hat{\eta} = \hat{k} Therefore it is possible to determine a vector along the direction perpendicular to the plane in which the actual velocities of the truck and the car lie.
