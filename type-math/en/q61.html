Question 61

BCIC College, Dhaka · Board question no. 5 · book p. 62

Three marbles — black, blue and red — on a flat table are set moving with velocities v1⃗=2i^+j^−k^\vec{v_1} = 2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}, v2⃗=3i^−2j^−4k^\vec{v_2} = 3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} - 4\hat{k} and v3⃗=i^−3j^+5k^\vec{v_3} = \hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k} respectively, and a collision occurs between them.

Part c · 3 marks

After the collision the red marble was at rest. Explain according to the stem.

Hint Try it first

Write the impulse as the change from initial to final momentum for the red marble.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know that the impulse of a force applied on a body (j⃗\vec{j}) is equal to the change in the momentum of the body. impulse of the force,
j⃗=ΔP3⃗\vec{j} = \Delta\vec{P_3}

j⃗=P3⃗′−P3⃗\vec{j} = \vec{P_3}' - \vec{P_3}
or, j⃗=m3v3⃗′−m3v3⃗\text{or, } \vec{j} = m_3\vec{v_3}' - m_3\vec{v_3}
or, j⃗=m3(0)−m3(i^−3j^+5k^)\text{or, } \vec{j} = m_3(0) - m_3(\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k})
or, j⃗=−m3i^+3m3j^−5m3k^\text{or, } \vec{j} = -m_3\hat{i} + 3m_3\hat{j} - 5m_3\hat{k}
After the collision the red marble was at rest, i.e. its final velocity is zero (v3⃗′=0\vec{v_3}' = 0). This means that the impulse (j⃗\vec{j}) applied to the red marble by the other two marbles (black and blue) during the collision was equal in magnitude to the red marble's initial momentum (P3⃗\vec{P_3}) and opposite in direction to that initial momentum. Initial momentum, P3⃗=m3(i^−3j^+5k^)\vec{P_3} = m_3(\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}). Then the impulse of the force, j⃗=−P3⃗\vec{j} = -\vec{P_3}. Therefore, as a result of the collision the red marble receives an impulse equal and opposite to its initial momentum, so that the velocity of the marble becomes zero and it comes to rest.

Part d · 4 marks

Is there a possibility that the marbles stay on the flat table after the collision? Explain with analysis.

Hint Try it first

Test the flat-table assumption for the initial velocities before predicting the final motion.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Whether the marbles were moving on the flat table depends on whether their velocity vectors lie in the same plane. If the three velocity vectors (v1⃗,v2⃗,v3⃗\vec{v_1}, \vec{v_2}, \vec{v_3}) are coplanar, their scalar triple product will be zero. Given, the velocity of the black marble v1⃗=2i^+j^−k^\vec{v_1} = 2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}, the velocity of the blue marble v2⃗=3i^−2j^−4k^\vec{v_2} = 3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} - 4\hat{k} and the velocity of the red marble v3⃗=i^−3j^+5k^\vec{v_3} = \hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k} then,
here, (v1⃗×v2⃗)⋅v3⃗=∣21−13−2−41−35∣\text{here, } (\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) \cdot \vec{v_3} = \left|\begin{matrix} 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 3 & -2 & -4 \\ 1 & -3 & 5 \end{matrix}\right|
2(−10−12)−1(15+4)−1(−9+2)=−44−19+72(-10 - 12) - 1(15 + 4) - 1(-9 + 2) = -44 - 19 + 7

2(−10−12)−1(15+4)−1(−9+2)=−562(-10 - 12) - 1(15 + 4) - 1(-9 + 2) = -56 Since the scalar triple product is not zero, i.e. (v1⃗×v2⃗)⋅v3⃗=−56≠0(\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) \cdot \vec{v_3} = -56 \neq 0, therefore the initial velocity vectors of the three marbles are not coplanar. Since the initial velocity vectors are not coplanar, their motion is three-dimensional. Even after the collision, according to the law of conservation of momentum, their motion will remain three-dimensional. Therefore, there is no possibility that the marbles will stay on the flat table after the collision.
