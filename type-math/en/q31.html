Question 31

Dinajpur Board · 2023 · Board question no. 4 · book p. 45

In the figure, one boatman is rowing a boat from point AA with velocity 16 kmh−116\text{ kmh}^{-1} at an angle 30∘30^\circ with ABAB, and another boatman from the same point with the same speed is rowing at an angle 60∘60^\circ with ABAB.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the resultant velocity of the first boatman.

Hint Try it first

Express the upstream component using the angle measured from AB.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know, resultant velocity,

w=u12+v2+2u1vcos⁡α=162+82+2×16×8cos⁡120∘=83 km h−1.\begin{aligned} w&=\sqrt{u_1^2+v^2+2u_1v\cos\alpha}\\ &=\sqrt{16^2+8^2+2\times16\times8\cos120^\circ}\\ &=8\sqrt3\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}. \end{aligned}
Here, the velocity of the 1st boatman's boat u1=16 km h−1u_1=16\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}; the velocity of the current v=8 km h−1v=8\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}; the angle between the boat and the current α=90∘+30∘=120∘\alpha=90^\circ+30^\circ=120^\circ; the resultant velocity of the first boatman w=?w=?.
Again,

tan⁡θ=u1sin⁡αv+u1cos⁡α\tan\theta=\frac{u_1\sin\alpha}{v+u_1\cos\alpha}
or,

θ=tan⁡−1u1sin⁡αv+u1cos⁡α\theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{u_1\sin\alpha}{v+u_1\cos\alpha}
or,

θ=tan⁡−116sin⁡120∘8+16cos⁡120∘\theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{16\sin120^\circ}{8+16\cos120^\circ}

∴θ=90∘.\therefore\theta=90^\circ.
∴ the magnitude of the resultant velocity is 8 m s⁻¹ and it makes an angle of 90° with the direction of the current. Ans. [the value and unit of the last sentence are as printed in the original]

Part d · 4 marks

Which boatman will cover the shorter distance — verify mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Calculate the net along-bank component of each boat; does either track have no drift?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

In the case of the 1st boatman, the required time,

t1=du1sin⁡120∘t_1=\frac{d}{u_1\sin120^\circ}

t1=216×32t_1 = \frac2{16\times\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}

t1=283 h.t_1 = \frac2{8\sqrt3}\ \mathrm h.
From (c) we get, the resultant velocity of the first boatman W1=83 km h−1W_1=8\sqrt3\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}; the velocity of the boatman u1=16 km h−1u_1=16\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}; the angle between them α=90∘+30∘=120∘\alpha=90^\circ+30^\circ=120^\circ; the required time t1=?t_1=?; the distance covered d1=?d_1=?; the width of the river d=2 kmd=2\ \mathrm{km}.
distance travelled,

d1=W1t1d_1=W_1t_1

d1=83×283 hd_1 = 8\sqrt3\times\frac2{8\sqrt3}\ \mathrm h

d1=2 km.d_1 = 2\ \mathrm{km}.
In the case of the 2nd boatman, resultant velocity,

w2=u22+v2+2u2vcos⁡α=162+82+2×16×8cos⁡150∘=9.91 km h−1.\begin{aligned} w_2&=\sqrt{u_2^2+v^2+2u_2v\cos\alpha}\\ &=\sqrt{16^2+8^2+2\times16\times8\cos150^\circ}\\ &=9.91\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}. \end{aligned}

Here, the velocity of the boatman u2=16 km h−1u_2=16\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}; the velocity of the current v=8 km h−1v=8\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}; the angle between them α=90∘+60∘=150∘\alpha=90^\circ+60^\circ=150^\circ; the resultant velocity w2=?w_2=?; the required time t2=?t_2=?; the distance covered d2=?d_2=?; the width of the river d=2 kmd=2\ \mathrm{km}.

d2=w2t2d_2=w_2t_2

d2=2.47 km.d_2 = 2.47\ \mathrm{km}.
Since d2>d1d_2>d_1, therefore the 1st boatman will cover the shorter distance to cross the river.
In the first calculation of (c) 83 km h−18\sqrt3\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}} is correct; the 8 m s⁻¹ in the last sentence is wrong. The correct value is about 13.86 km h⁻¹ or 3.85 m s⁻¹. In (d) the h unit in the product of d1d_1 has been placed loosely without writing the full units of velocity and time; the result 2 km is correct. The second resultant is more precisely 9.9145 km h⁻¹ and the path 2.4786 km (2.48 km to two decimals); the original's 2.47 is rounded prematurely.
