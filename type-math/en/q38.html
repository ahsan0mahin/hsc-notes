Part d · 4 marks

Verify, through mathematical analysis, which of the two vectors A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B} described in the stem is solenoidal and which is irrotational.

Hint Try it first

A divergence tests one condition; a curl tests the other. Decide which operation produces a scalar.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

given, A⃗=(2x+y)i^+(3y+z2)j^+(−5z+x)k^\vec{A} = (2x + y)\hat{i} + (3y + z^2)\hat{j} + (-5z + x)\hat{k} the divergence of A⃗\vec{A} div A⃗\text{div}\,\vec{A}
<div class="math">∇⃗⋅A⃗=(∂∂xi^+∂∂yj^+∂∂zk^)⋅{(2x+y)i^+(3y+z2)j^+(−5z+x)k^}\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{A} = \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\hat{i} + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\hat{j} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\hat{k}\right) \cdot \{(2x + y)\hat{i} + (3y + z^2)\hat{j} + (-5z + x)\hat{k}\}</div>
∂∂x(2x+y)+∂∂y(3y+z2)+∂∂z(−5z+x)\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(2x + y) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(3y + z^2) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(-5z + x)
2+3−5=02 + 3 - 5 = 0 Since ∇⃗⋅A⃗=0\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{A} = 0, therefore the vector A⃗\vec{A} is solenoidal.
the curl of A⃗\vec{A} curl A⃗=∇⃗×A⃗\text{curl}\,\vec{A} = \vec{\nabla} \times \vec{A}
<div class="math">∣i^j^k^∂∂x∂∂y∂∂z2x+y3y+z2−5z+x∣\left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ 2x + y & 3y + z^2 & -5z + x \end{matrix}\right|</div>
<div class="math">i^{∂∂y(−5z+x)−∂∂z(3y+z2)}−j^{∂∂x(−5z+x)−∂∂z(2x+y)}+k^{∂∂x(3y+z2)−∂∂y(2x+y)}\hat{i}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(-5z + x) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(3y + z^2)\right\} - \hat{j}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(-5z + x) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(2x + y)\right\} + \hat{k}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(3y + z^2) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(2x + y)\right\}</div>
i^{0−2z}−j^{1−0}+k^{0−1}=−2zi^−j^−k^\hat{i}\{0 - 2z\} - \hat{j}\{1 - 0\} + \hat{k}\{0 - 1\} = -2z\hat{i} - \hat{j} - \hat{k} Since ∇⃗×A⃗≠0⃗\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{A} \neq \vec{0}, therefore the vector A⃗\vec{A} is rotational
Since B⃗=(6xy+z3)i^+(3x2−z)j^+(3xz2−y)k^\vec{B} = (6xy + z^3)\hat{i} + (3x^2 - z)\hat{j} + (3xz^2 - y)\hat{k} the divergence of B⃗\vec{B} div B⃗\text{div}\,\vec{B}
<div class="math">∇⃗⋅B⃗=(∂∂xi^+∂∂yj^+∂∂zk^)⋅{(6xy+z3)i^+(3x2−z)j^+(3xz2−y)k^}\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{B} = \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\hat{i} + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\hat{j} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\hat{k}\right) \cdot \{(6xy + z^3)\hat{i} + (3x^2 - z)\hat{j} + (3xz^2 - y)\hat{k}\}</div>
∂∂x(6xy+z3)+∂∂y(3x2−z)+∂∂z(3xz2−y)\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(6xy + z^3) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(3x^2 - z) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(3xz^2 - y)
6y+0+6xz=6xz+6y6y + 0 + 6xz = 6xz + 6y Since ∇⃗⋅B⃗≠0\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{B} \neq 0, therefore the vector B⃗\vec{B} is not solenoidal.
the curl of B⃗\vec{B} curl B⃗=∇⃗×B⃗\text{curl}\,\vec{B} = \vec{\nabla} \times \vec{B}
<div class="math">∣i^j^k^∂∂x∂∂y∂∂z6xy+z33x2−z3xz2−y∣\left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ 6xy + z^3 & 3x^2 - z & 3xz^2 - y \end{matrix}\right|</div>
<div class="math">i^{∂∂y(3xz2−y)−∂∂z(3x2−z)}−j^{∂∂x(3xz2−y)−∂∂z(6xy+z3)}+k^{∂∂x(3x2−z)−∂∂y(6xy+z3)}\hat{i}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(3xz^2 - y) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(3x^2 - z)\right\} - \hat{j}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(3xz^2 - y) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(6xy + z^3)\right\} + \hat{k}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(3x^2 - z) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(6xy + z^3)\right\}</div>
i^(−1+1)−j^(3z2−3z2)+k^(6x−6x)\hat{i}(-1 + 1) - \hat{j}(3z^2 - 3z^2) + \hat{k}(6x - 6x)
0i^−0j^+0k^=00\hat{i} - 0\hat{j} + 0\hat{k} = 0 Since ∇⃗×B⃗=0⃗\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{B} = \vec{0}, therefore the vector B⃗\vec{B} is irrotational. that is, the vector A⃗\vec{A} is solenoidal and the vector B⃗\vec{B} is irrotational.
