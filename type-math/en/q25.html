Part c · 3 marks

Determine the vector form of F1F_1.

Hint Try it first

F₁ is in quadrant II. Which component must be negative?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

horizontal component of the force F⃗1\vec{F}_1
∣F⃗1∣cos⁡θ=15cos⁡150∘|\vec{F}_1|\cos\theta = 15\cos 150^\circ

∣F⃗1∣cos⁡θ=−15⋅32|\vec{F}_1|\cos\theta = -15 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} vertical component of the force F⃗1\vec{F}_1 = ∣F⃗1∣sin⁡θ|\vec{F}_1|\sin\theta
15sin⁡150∘=15×1215\sin 150^\circ = 15 \times \frac{1}{2}

15sin⁡150∘=15215\sin 150^\circ = \frac{15}{2}
Therefore, the vector form of F1F_1, F⃗1=−1532i^+152j^\vec{F}_1 = -\frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{15}{2}\hat{j} Ans.

Part d · 4 marks

Are F⃗1+F⃗2\vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2 and F⃗1−F⃗2\vec{F}_1 - \vec{F}_2 in the stem mutually perpendicular? Verify.

Hint Try it first

Expand the dot product of the sum and difference; the mixed products cancel.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Here, the vector form of F2F_2,
F⃗2=10cos⁡30∘i^+10sin⁡30∘j^\vec{F}_2 = 10\cos 30^\circ\hat{i} + 10\sin 30^\circ\hat{j}

F⃗2=1032i^+10×12j^\vec{F}_2 = \frac{10\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + 10 \times \frac{1}{2}\hat{j}

F⃗2=53i^+5j^\vec{F}_2 = 5\sqrt{3}\hat{i} + 5\hat{j}
now, F⃗1+F⃗2=−1532i^+152j^+53i^+5j^=−532i^+252j^\vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2 = -\frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{15}{2}\hat{j} + 5\sqrt{3}\hat{i} + 5\hat{j} = -\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{25}{2}\hat{j} Here, F⃗1−F⃗2=−1532i^+152j^−53i^−5j^=−2532i^+52j^\vec{F}_1 - \vec{F}_2 = -\frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{15}{2}\hat{j} - 5\sqrt{3}\hat{i} - 5\hat{j} = -\frac{25\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{5}{2}\hat{j}
(F⃗1+F⃗2)⋅(F⃗1−F⃗2)=(−532i^+252j^)⋅(−2532i^+52j^)=125×(3)24+1254=125≠0(\vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2) \cdot (\vec{F}_1 - \vec{F}_2) = \left(-\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{25}{2}\hat{j}\right) \cdot \left(-\frac{25\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{i} + \frac{5}{2}\hat{j}\right) = \frac{125 \times (\sqrt{3})^2}{4} + \frac{125}{4} = 125 \neq 0 Since the dot product is not zero, the two vectors (F⃗1+F⃗2)(\vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2) and (F⃗1−F⃗2)(\vec{F}_1 - \vec{F}_2) are not mutually perpendicular.
