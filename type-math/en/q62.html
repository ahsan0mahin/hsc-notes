Dhaka Imperial College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 62

P⃗=5i^+3j^−mk^\vec{P} = 5\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} - m\hat{k}, Q⃗=i^+j^+4k^\vec{Q} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} + 4\hat{k}, here P⃗\vec{P} and Q⃗\vec{Q} are mutually perpendicular. If the magnitudes of P⃗\vec{P} and Q⃗\vec{Q} represent the speeds of a boat and of the current in a river respectively, then the boat takes 22 minutes to cross the river along the shortest path.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the value of 'mm'.

Hint Try it first

Take the dot product using the unknown z component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

8−4m=08-4m=0

P·Q=8−4m=0, hence m=2.

Part d · 4 marks

If the boatman wants to cross the river in the minimum time, will he cover a distance greater than the width of the river? Give your opinion.

Hint Try it first

Convert two minutes to seconds if using m/s. Distinguish minimum-time heading from minimum-path heading.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

D=12020 m,S=D56/38(v in m/s)D=120\sqrt{20}\,\mathrm m,\quad S=D\sqrt{56/38}\quad (v\text{ in m/s})

The speeds are √38 and √18. The direct crossing speed is √20; the minimum-time path-to-width ratio is √56/√38≈1.214. The route is longer.
