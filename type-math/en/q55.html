Question 55

Dhaka College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 58

A boat is moving with velocity 10ms−110\text{ms}^{-1} in a river 360 m360\text{ m} wide. Crossing the river, the boat reached point CC on the opposite bank. The velocity of the current in the river is 3ms−13\text{ms}^{-1}.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the distance BCBC on the opposite bank of the river.

Hint Try it first

Use the 37° heading to get crossing time. Does the current contribute to BC or to the width?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

BC=360(3+10cos⁡37∘)10sin⁡37∘BC=\frac{360(3+10\cos37^\circ)}{10\sin37^\circ}

Interpreting 37° as the heading relative to water, time=360/(10 sin37°) s. Multiply by 3+10 cos37° to get BC≈657.2 m.
