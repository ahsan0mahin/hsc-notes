Question 44

Mirzapur Cadet College, Tangail · Board question no. 7 · book p. 52

OACBOACB is a parallelogram, with angles AOB=30∘AOB = 30^\circ, OA→=P⃗\overrightarrow{OA} = \vec{P}, OB→=Q⃗\overrightarrow{OB} = \vec{Q}. The magnitudes of P⃗\vec{P} and Q⃗\vec{Q} are 55 and 33 respectively.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the value of P⃗−Q⃗\vec{P} - \vec{Q}.

Hint Try it first

Use the angle between P and Q in the cosine rule for their difference.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know,
R=P2+Q2−2PQcos⁡αR = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2 - 2PQ\cos\alpha}
52+32−2×5×3×cos⁡30∘\sqrt{5^2 + 3^2 - 2 \times 5 \times 3 \times \cos 30^\circ}
25+9−30×0.866\sqrt{25 + 9 - 30 \times 0.866}
∴R=2.83 Ans.\therefore R = 2.83\text{ Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

Show mathematically that the area of triangle AOCAOC = the area of triangle ++ = the area of triangle BOCBOC = the area of parallelogram = OACBOACB.

Hint Try it first

What does a diagonal do to the area of a parallelogram? Verify using base and height.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

In the case of the parallelogram OACB in the stem, OA→=P⃗\overrightarrow{OA}=\vec P, OB→=Q⃗\overrightarrow{OB}=\vec Q.
Diagonal of the parallelogram, OC→=P⃗+Q⃗\overrightarrow{OC}=\vec P+\vec Q.
Area of parallelogram OACB,

A=∣P⃗×Q⃗∣A=|\vec P\times\vec Q|
or, A=PQsin⁡αA=PQ\sin\alpha or, A=5×3×sin⁡30∘A=5\times3\times\sin30^\circ or, A=15×0.5A=15\times0.5

∴A=7.5 square units.\therefore A=7.5\ \text{square units}.

Area of ΔAOC,

<div class="math">A1=12∣OA→×OC→∣=12∣P⃗×(P⃗+Q⃗)∣=12∣(P⃗×P⃗)+P⃗×Q⃗∣=12∣0+(P⃗+Q⃗)∣[∵P⃗×P⃗=0][in this line of the original a plus sign appears instead of a multiplication sign]=12∣P⃗×Q⃗∣=12×7.5.\begin{aligned} A_1&=\frac12|\overrightarrow{OA}\times\overrightarrow{OC}|\\ &=\frac12|\vec P\times(\vec P+\vec Q)|=\frac12|(\vec P\times\vec P)+\vec P\times\vec Q|\\ &=\frac12|0+(\vec P+\vec Q)|\quad[\because\vec P\times\vec P=0]\quad\text{[in this line of the original a plus sign appears instead of a multiplication sign]}\\ &=\frac12|\vec P\times\vec Q|=\frac12\times7.5. \end{aligned}</div>

∴A1=3.75 square units.\therefore A_1=3.75\ \text{square units}.

∴A2=3.75 square units.\therefore A_2=3.75\ \text{square units}.
Now, area of ΔAOC + area of ΔBOC

A1+A2=3.75+3.75A_1+A_2=3.75+3.75

Mathematical analysis shows that A1+A2=AA_1+A_2=A, that is, area of ΔAOC + area of ΔBOC = area of parallelogram OACB.
