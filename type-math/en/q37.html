Three points O(0,0,0)O(0, 0, 0), P(2,4,2)P(2, 4, 2) and Q(2,−4,−4)Q(2, -4, -4) in a Cartesian coordinate system.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the value of PQ→\overrightarrow{PQ}.

Hint Try it first

Compute the change in all three coordinates before taking the length.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Here, the coordinates of point P are P(2, 4, 2); the coordinates of point Q are Q(2, −4, −4).
Now, the position vector of point P,

OP→=2i^+4j^+2k^\overrightarrow{OP}=2\hat i+4\hat j+2\hat k
and the position vector of point Q,

OQ→=2i^−4j^−4k^.\overrightarrow{OQ}=2\hat i-4\hat j-4\hat k.

∴PQ→=OQ→−OP→\therefore\overrightarrow{PQ}=\overrightarrow{OQ}-\overrightarrow{OP}

PQ→=2i^−4j^−4k^−2i^−4j^−2k^\overrightarrow{PQ} = 2\hat i-4\hat j-4\hat k-2\hat i-4\hat j-2\hat k

PQ→=−8j^−6k^\overrightarrow{PQ} = -8\hat j-6\hat k

∴∣PQ→∣=(−8)2+(−6)2\therefore|\overrightarrow{PQ}|=\sqrt{(-8)^2+(-6)^2}

∣PQ→∣=64+36|\overrightarrow{PQ}| = \sqrt{64+36}

∣PQ→∣=100|\overrightarrow{PQ}| = \sqrt{100}

∣PQ→∣=10.(Ans.)|\overrightarrow{PQ}| = 10.\quad(\mathrm{Ans.})

Part d · 4 marks

Verify whether the position vectors PP and QQ are mutually perpendicular.

Hint Try it first

Keep both position vectors based at O when taking their dot product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(2,4,2)⋅(2,−4,−4)=−20(2,4,2)\cdot(2,-4,-4)=-20

OP·OQ=4−16−8=−20. A nonzero dot product means the vectors are not perpendicular.
