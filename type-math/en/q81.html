Gradient–divergence–curl

Question 81

Sylhet Cantonment Public School & College · Board question no. 4 · book p. 70

A bus, a jeep and a truck are moving along a road. Their velocities are respectively — v1⃗=2i^+j^−k^,  v2⃗=3i^−2j^+4k^\vec{v_1} = 2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k},\; \vec{v_2} = 3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 4\hat{k} and v3⃗=i^−3j^+5k^\vec{v_3} = \hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}. At some instant a collision occurs among the three.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the curl of the velocity of the truck.

Hint Try it first

The given velocity components are constants. Consider their derivatives with respect to position.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Given, Curl(v3⃗)=∇⃗×v3⃗\text{Curl}(\vec{v_3}) = \vec{\nabla} \times \vec{v_3}
<div class="math">(∂∂xi^+∂∂yj^+∂∂zk^)×(i^−3j^+5k^)\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\hat{i} + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\hat{j} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\hat{k}\right) \times (\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k})</div>
<div class="math">∣i^j^k^∂∂x∂∂y∂∂z1−35∣\left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ 1 & -3 & 5 \end{matrix}\right|</div>
<div class="math">i^{∂∂y(5)−∂∂z(−3)}−j^{∂∂z(5)−∂∂x(1)}+k^{∂∂x(−3)−∂∂y(1)}\hat{i}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(5) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(-3)\right\} - \hat{j}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial z}(5) - \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(1)\right\} + \hat{k}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(-3) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(1)\right\}</div>
i^(0−0)−j^(0−0)+k^(0−0)\hat{i}(0 - 0) - \hat{j}(0 - 0) + \hat{k}(0 - 0)
∴Curl(v3⃗)=0 Ans.\therefore \text{Curl}(\vec{v_3}) = 0\text{ Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

Prove mathematically that at the moment of collision the three vehicles were moving in the same plane.

Hint Try it first

Compute the scalar triple product; a vanishing value tests coplanarity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The three vectors will lie in the same plane if (v1⃗×v2⃗)⋅v3⃗=0(\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) \cdot \vec{v_3} = 0. here, (v1⃗×v2⃗)=(2i^+j^−k^)×(3i^−2j^+4k^)(\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) = (2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}) \times (3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 4\hat{k})
∣i^j^k^21−13−24∣=(4−2)i^−(8+3)j^+(−4−3)k^=2i^−11j^−7k^\left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 3 & -2 & 4 \end{matrix}\right| = (4 - 2)\hat{i} - (8 + 3)\hat{j} + (-4 - 3)\hat{k} = 2\hat{i} - 11\hat{j} - 7\hat{k} Since (v1⃗×v2⃗)⋅v3⃗=(2i^−11j^−7k^)⋅(i^−3j^+5k^)=2+33−35=0(\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) \cdot \vec{v_3} = (2\hat{i} - 11\hat{j} - 7\hat{k}) \cdot (\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}) = 2 + 33 - 35 = 0
∴(v1⃗×v2⃗)⋅v3⃗=0\therefore (\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) \cdot \vec{v_3} = 0 Since (v1⃗×v2⃗)⋅v3⃗=0(\vec{v_1} \times \vec{v_2}) \cdot \vec{v_3} = 0, therefore the three vectors lie in the same plane. Therefore, at the moment of collision the three vehicles were moving in the same plane.
