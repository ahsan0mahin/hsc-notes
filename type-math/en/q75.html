River–boat and current

Question 75

Ispahani Cantonment Public School & College, Cumilla · Board question no. 1 · book p. 68

Kamal was rowing a boat at an angle 40∘40^\circ with the current in a river. The velocities of his boat and of the current were 10ms−110\text{ms}^{-1} and 4ms−14\text{ms}^{-1} respectively. The width of the river was 40m40\text{m}.

Part c · 3 marks

If the river is crossed diagonally, determine the distance covered by Kamal.

Hint Try it first

Find the crossing time using the perpendicular component. Multiply by the actual ground speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Crossing time=40/(10 sin40°). Ground speed is √((4+10 cos40°)²+(10 sin40°)²); multiplying gives about 82.85 m.
