River–boat and current

Question 84

Government Prafulla Chandra College, Bagerhat · Board question no. 2 · book p. 71

A boatman starts from point AA on the bank of a river 5 km5\text{ km} wide, rowing a boat with velocity 16 kmh−116\text{ kmh}^{-1}, in order to cross to the other bank. A man waited at point BB on the opposite bank for 2525 minutes from the start of the boat and then left. The velocity of the current in the river is 8 kmh−18\text{ kmh}^{-1}.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the resultant velocity of the boat.

Hint Try it first

For the initial square crossing add current and boat velocities at right angles.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=162+82,tan⁡θ=16/8R=\sqrt{16^2+8^2},\quad\tan\theta=16/8

Assuming an initial heading squarely across, ground speed is √(16²+8²)≈17.9 km/h at 63.4° to downstream.

Part d · 4 marks

How must the boat be rowed to reach point BB straight across on the other bank? Did the boatman meet the waiting man? Analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Landing directly opposite imposes zero drift; use the remaining component to test the 25 min limit.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡α=−8/16,t=516sin⁡120∘ h\cos\alpha=-8/16,\quad t=\frac{5}{16\sin120^\circ}\,\mathrm h

Steer at arccos(−8/16)=120° to the current. Crossing time=60×5/(16 sin120°)≈21.65 min, within the waiting period.
