The coordinates of three points PP, QQ and RR are P(2,−1,3)P(2, -1, 3), Q(3,−1,2)Q(3, -1, 2) and R(1,−3,5)R(1, -3, 5) respectively.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the angle between PQ→\overrightarrow{PQ} and PR→\overrightarrow{PR}.

Hint Try it first

Build both displacement vectors from the common vertex P.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡θ=(1,0,−1)⋅(−1,−2,2)2⋅3\cos\theta=\frac{(1,0,-1)\cdot(-1,-2,2)}{\sqrt2\cdot3}

PQ=(1,0,−1) and PR=(−1,−2,2). Their dot product is −3, so cos θ=−1/√2 and θ=135°.
