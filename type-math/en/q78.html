Full solution (added):

From V⃗⋅B⃗=6+6−2x=0\vec V\cdot\vec B=6+6-2x=0, x = 6. The boat's speed is 7 and the current's speed is 17\sqrt{17}. Taking the time in seconds and the speed in m s⁻¹, the width is D=24049−17=24032≈1357.645D=240\sqrt{49-17}=240\sqrt{32}\approx1357.645 m from the 240 s taken along the shortest path. The distance covered in the minimum time is S=D49+17/7≈1575.651S=D\sqrt{49+17}/7\approx1575.651 m, which is greater than D. The unit of velocity is not clearly printed in the stem, so the metre unit for distance rests on this assumption. The definition of an antiparallel vector applies when the original vector is non-zero.
