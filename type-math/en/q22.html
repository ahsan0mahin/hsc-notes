Question 22

Chattogram Board · 2024 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 41

The velocity of Sujan's boat along ABAB is = 6 ms−16\text{ ms}^{-1}
The velocity of Kuber's boat along ACAC is = 5.5 ms−15.5\text{ ms}^{-1}
The velocity of the current along CBCB is = 1.2 ms−11.2\text{ ms}^{-1}
Width of the river, AB=400 mAB = 400\text{ m}.

Part c · 3 marks

At what magnitude of velocity did Kuber's boat actually reach the other bank? Determine.

Hint Try it first

The angle between the boat’s heading and the current is obtuse. Check the sign of the cross term in the squared speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=5.52+1.22+2(5.5)(1.2)cos⁡120∘R=\sqrt{5.5^2+1.2^2+2(5.5)(1.2)\cos120^\circ}

Adding the velocities gives 5.52+1.22+2(5.5)(1.2)cos⁡120∘≈5.01\sqrt{5.5^2+1.2^2+2(5.5)(1.2)\cos120^\circ}\approx5.01 m/s.

Part d · 4 marks

Between Sujan and Kuber, who will reach the other bank first? Give your opinion with mathematical analysis.

Hint Try it first

Both boats cross the same width; compare their perpendicular speed components.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

If the time required for Kuber to cross the river is t1t_1, then
t1=du1sin⁡α1t_1 = \frac{d}{u_1\sin\alpha_1}

t1=4005.5sin⁡120∘t_1 = \frac{400}{5.5\sin 120^\circ}

t1=83.98 st_1 = 83.98\text{ s} Now, if the time required for Sujan to cross the river is t2t_2, then
t2=du2sin⁡α2t_2 = \frac{d}{u_2\sin\alpha_2}

t2=4006sin⁡90∘t_2 = \frac{400}{6\sin 90^\circ}

t2=66.67 st_2 = 66.67\text{ s}
or, The angle of Kuber's moving boat with the direction of the current while crossing the river, α1=120∘\alpha_1 = 120^\circ Width of the river, d=400 md = 400\text{ m} Velocity of Kuber's boat, u1=5.5 ms−1u_1 = 5.5\text{ ms}^{-1} Velocity of Sujan's boat, u2=6 ms−1u_2 = 6\text{ ms}^{-1} Angle of Sujan's boat velocity with the direction of the current, α2=90∘\alpha_2 = 90^\circ
It is seen that t2<t1t_2 < t_1 Therefore it can be said that between Sujan and Kuber, Sujan will reach the other bank first.
