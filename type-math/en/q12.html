Part c · 3 marks

Determine the projection of B⃗\vec{B} along A⃗\vec{A}.

Hint Try it first

Make A into a unit vector before projecting B.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

compAB=−2+6+3538\mathrm{comp}_A B=\frac{-2+6+35}{\sqrt{38}}

A·B=39 and A has length √38. The scalar projection is 39/√38 ≈ 6.33.

(d). We know that three vectors will be coplanar if A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=0\vec A\cdot(\vec B\times\vec C)=0.

Now,

B⃗×C⃗=(−i^+2j^+7k^)×(i^+7j^−k^)=∣i^j^k^−12717−1∣=i^(−2−49)−j^(1−7)+k^(−7−2)=−51i^+6j^−9k^.\begin{aligned} \vec B\times\vec C &=(-\hat i+2\hat j+7\hat k)\times(\hat i+7\hat j-\hat k)\\ &=\left|\begin{matrix}\hat i&\hat j&\hat k\\-1&2&7\\1&7&-1\end{matrix}\right|\\ &=\hat i(-2-49)-\hat j(1-7)+\hat k(-7-2)\\ &=-51\hat i+6\hat j-9\hat k. \end{aligned}

Here,

A⃗=2i^+3j^+5k^,\vec A=2\hat i+3\hat j+5\hat k,
B⃗=−i^+2j^+7k^,\vec B=-\hat i+2\hat j+7\hat k,

∴B⃗×C⃗=−51i^+6j^−9k^.\therefore\vec B\times\vec C=-51\hat i+6\hat j-9\hat k.

Since in the case of the vectors A⃗\vec A, B⃗\vec B and C⃗\vec C of the stem A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=−129\vec A\cdot(\vec B\times\vec C)=-129, it can be said that the three vectors A⃗\vec A, B⃗\vec B and C⃗\vec C will not lie in the same plane.
