Dhaka Board · 2024 · Board question no. 2 · book p. 36

In the figure above, Tamal is rowing a boat from point 'P' towards the other bank of the river. Rowing along PSPS with a velocity of 5 kmh−15\text{ kmh}^{-1}, he reaches point MM on the opposite bank. [width of the river PQ=2 kmPQ = 2\text{ km}]

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the distance of QMQM.

Hint Try it first

Find the crossing time from the perpendicular component. Multiply that time by the net downstream speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Let the resultant velocity make an angle θ\theta with the velocity of the current.
∴tan⁡θ=vsin⁡αu+vcos⁡α\therefore \tan\theta = \frac{v\sin\alpha}{u + v\cos\alpha}
or, tan⁡θ=5sin⁡100∘3+5cos⁡100∘\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{5\sin 100^\circ}{3 + 5\cos 100^\circ}
or, tan⁡θ=2.31\text{or, } \tan\theta = 2.31
or, θ=tan⁡−1(2.31)\text{or, } \theta = \tan^{-1}(2.31)
∴θ=66.59∘\therefore \theta = 66.59^\circ

Part d · 4 marks

Determine, with mathematical analysis, the difference between the time taken to reach point 'M' by rowing the boat along PSPS and the time taken to reach QQ by rowing the boat directly from PP in the absence of a current.

Hint Try it first

Use the same width twice but different perpendicular speeds. Subtract before rounding to seconds.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

reaching point MM by rowing the boat along PSPS
required time, t1=dvsin⁡α\text{required time, } t_1 = \frac{d}{v\sin\alpha}

required time, t1=25sin⁡100∘\text{required time, } t_1 = \frac{2}{5\sin 100^\circ}

required time, t1=0.406 h\text{required time, } t_1 = 0.406\text{ h}

required time, t1=24.35 min\text{required time, } t_1 = 24.35\text{ min}
Time to reach QQ by rowing the boat from PP in the absence of a current: In this case the velocity of the boat will be along PQPQ Therefore, α=90∘\alpha = 90^\circ
∴required time, t2=25sin⁡90∘\therefore \text{required time, } t_2 = \frac{2}{5\sin 90^\circ}

required time, t2=0.4 h\text{required time, } t_2 = 0.4\text{ h}

required time, t2=24 min\text{required time, } t_2 = 24\text{ min}
∴t2<t1\therefore t_2 < t_1
difference in time=t1−t2\text{difference in time} = t_1 - t_2
