Question 17

Rajshahi Board · 2024 · Board question no. 4 · book p. 38

According to the figure, three forces are applied on a stationary particle at point OO.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the magnitude of the resultant force acting on the particle along OXOX.

Hint Try it first

Take east as positive. Express all directions relative to +x before summing horizontal components.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Let the resultant force F⃗\vec{F} make an angle θ\theta with the direction of OXOX. the angle of F⃗1\vec{F}_1 with OXOX, θ1=0∘\theta_1 = 0^\circ the angle of F⃗2\vec{F}_2 with OXOX, θ2=180∘−60∘=120∘\theta_2 = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ the angle of F⃗3\vec{F}_3 with OXOX, θ3=270∘−30∘=240∘\theta_3 = 270^\circ - 30^\circ = 240^\circ
∴\therefore the magnitude of the resultant force acting on the particle along OXOX,
Fx=∣F⃗∣cos⁡θF_x = |\vec{F}|\cos\theta

Fx=∣F⃗1∣cos⁡θ1+∣F⃗2∣cos⁡θ2+∣F⃗3∣cos⁡θ3F_x = |\vec{F}_1|\cos\theta_1 + |\vec{F}_2|\cos\theta_2 + |\vec{F}_3|\cos\theta_3
Fx=11cos⁡0∘+22cos⁡120∘+33cos⁡240∘F_x = 11\cos 0^\circ + 22\cos 120^\circ + 33\cos 240^\circ
−16.5 N Ans.-16.5\text{ N Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

In which direction will the particle move as a result of the applied force? Give your opinion with mathematical analysis.

Hint Try it first

Find the vertical component as well. The signs of both components determine the correct quadrant.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Obtained from (c), Fx=−16.5 N⋯(i)F_x=-16.5\ \mathrm N\quad\cdots(i).
similarly,

<div class="math">Fy=∣F⃗∣sin⁡θ=∣F⃗1∣sin⁡θ1+∣F⃗2∣sin⁡θ2+∣F⃗3∣sin⁡θ3=11sin⁡0∘+22sin⁡120∘+33sin⁡240∘=−1132 N⋯(ii).\begin{aligned} F_y&=|\vec F|\sin\theta=|\vec F_1|\sin\theta_1+|\vec F_2|\sin\theta_2+|\vec F_3|\sin\theta_3\\ &=11\sin0^\circ+22\sin120^\circ+33\sin240^\circ\\ &=-\frac{11\sqrt3}{2}\ \mathrm N\quad\cdots(ii). \end{aligned}</div>
here, ∣F⃗1∣=11 N|\vec F_1|=11\ \mathrm N; ∣F⃗2∣=22 N|\vec F_2|=22\ \mathrm N; ∣F⃗3∣=33 N|\vec F_3|=33\ \mathrm N.
Dividing (ii) by (i) we get,

FxFy=∣F⃗∣sin⁡θ∣F⃗∣cos⁡θ\frac{F_x}{F_y}=\frac{|\vec F|\sin\theta}{|\vec F|\cos\theta}

FxFy=−1132−16.5.[left-hand side of the original]\frac{F_x}{F_y} = \frac{-\frac{11\sqrt3}{2}}{-16.5}.\quad\text{[left-hand side of the original]}
or,

tan⁡θ=13\tan\theta=\frac1{\sqrt3}

∴θ=210∘.\therefore\theta=210^\circ.
(Since sin⁡θ\sin\theta, both cos⁡θ\cos\theta are negative, so θ\theta lies in the 3rd quadrant.)
Therefore, as a result of the applied force the particle will move at an angle of 210° with the direction of OX; in other words, at an angle of (210° − 180°) = 30° downwards with the direction of OX′.
On the division line the left-hand side is printed as Fx/FyF_x/F_y, but according to the right-hand side and the division indicated it should be Fy/FxF_y/F_x. The angle 210° and the magnitudes of both components are correct. In (c) by 'magnitude' the signed component −16.5 N is meant; the absolute value is 16.5 N, direction OX′.
