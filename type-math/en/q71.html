The 1st boatman crosses the river by rowing along AEAE and the 2nd boatman along ACAC. The velocity of both boats is 12km/h12\text{km/h} and the width of the river is 5km5\text{km}.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the horizontal distance travelled by the first boatman along the river.

Hint Try it first

The along-bank drift accumulates throughout the time needed to cover the width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

x=5(8+12cos⁡45∘)12sin⁡45∘x=\frac{5(8+12\cos45^\circ)}{12\sin45^\circ}

Time=5/(12 sin45°) h. Downstream displacement=(8+12 cos45°) times that time, about 9.71 km.
