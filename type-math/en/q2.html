Question 2

Rajshahi Board · 2025 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 30

A scalar field ϕ=ϕ(x,y,z)\phi = \phi(x, y, z), a vector field V⃗=(Vxi^+Vyj^+Vzk^)\vec{V} = (V_x\hat{i} + V_y\hat{j} + V_z\hat{k}) and a vector operator ∇⃗=∂∂xi^+∂∂yj^+∂∂zk^\vec{\nabla} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\hat{i} + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\hat{j} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\hat{k}

[book printing error: in the book the operator is printed with \vec{V} and a partial-differentiation \delta sign]

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the value of ∇⃗⋅(∇⃗×V⃗)\vec{\nabla} \cdot (\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{V}).

Hint Try it first

Expand the three terms of the divergence after taking the curl. Pair mixed derivatives in opposite orders.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

given, V⃗=Vxi^+Vyj^+Vzk^\vec{V} = V_x\hat{i} + V_y\hat{j} + V_z\hat{k} Now,
<div class="math">∇⃗×V⃗=∣i^j^k^δδxδδyδδzVxVyVz∣\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{V} = \left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \frac{\delta}{\delta x} & \frac{\delta}{\delta y} & \frac{\delta}{\delta z} \\ V_x & V_y & V_z \end{matrix}\right|</div>
i^(δδyVz−δδzVy)−j^(δδxVz−δδzVx)+k^(δδxVy−δδyVx)\hat{i}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta y}V_z - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}V_y\right) - \hat{j}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta x}V_z - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}V_x\right) + \hat{k}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta x}V_y - \frac{\delta}{\delta y}V_x\right) Now,
<div class="math">∇⃗⋅(∇⃗×V⃗)=(i^δδx+j^δδy+k^δδz)⋅{i^(δδyVz−δδzVy)−j^(δδxVz−δδzVx)+k^(δδxVy−δδyVx)}\vec{\nabla} \cdot (\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{V}) = \left(\hat{i}\frac{\delta}{\delta x} + \hat{j}\frac{\delta}{\delta y} + \hat{k}\frac{\delta}{\delta z}\right) \cdot \left\{\hat{i}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta y}V_z - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}V_y\right) - \hat{j}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta x}V_z - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}V_x\right) + \hat{k}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta x}V_y - \frac{\delta}{\delta y}V_x\right)\right\}</div>
<div class="math">δδx(δδyVz−δδzVy)−δδy(δδxVz−δδzVx)+δδz(δδxVy−δδyVx)\frac{\delta}{\delta x}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta y}V_z - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}V_y\right) - \frac{\delta}{\delta y}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta x}V_z - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}V_x\right) + \frac{\delta}{\delta z}\left(\frac{\delta}{\delta x}V_y - \frac{\delta}{\delta y}V_x\right)</div>

Part d · 4 marks

Will the curl of the gradient of ϕ\phi in the stem be zero? Analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

Take the gradient first. Examine the two mixed derivatives in each component of its curl.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

given, ϕ=ϕ(x,y,z)\phi = \phi(x, y, z) the gradient of ϕ\phi ∇⃗ϕ=(i^δδx+j^δδy+k^δδz)ϕ\vec{\nabla}\phi = \left(\hat{i}\frac{\delta}{\delta x} + \hat{j}\frac{\delta}{\delta y} + \hat{k}\frac{\delta}{\delta z}\right)\phi
i^δϕδx+j^δϕδy+k^δϕδz\hat{i}\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta x} + \hat{j}\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta y} + \hat{k}\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta z}
ϕ\phi and ∇⃗×(∇⃗ϕ)\vec{\nabla} \times (\vec{\nabla}\phi) are two vectors.
<div class="math">∣i^j^k^δδxδδyδδzδϕδxδϕδyδϕδz∣\left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \frac{\delta}{\delta x} & \frac{\delta}{\delta y} & \frac{\delta}{\delta z} \\ \frac{\delta\phi}{\delta x} & \frac{\delta\phi}{\delta y} & \frac{\delta\phi}{\delta z} \end{matrix}\right|</div>
<div class="math">i^{δδy(δϕδz)−δδz(δϕδy)}−j^{δδx(δϕδz)−δδz(δϕδx)}+k^{δδx(δϕδy)−δδy(δϕδx)}\hat{i}\left\{\frac{\delta}{\delta y}\left(\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta z}\right) - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}\left(\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta y}\right)\right\} - \hat{j}\left\{\frac{\delta}{\delta x}\left(\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta z}\right) - \frac{\delta}{\delta z}\left(\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta x}\right)\right\} + \hat{k}\left\{\frac{\delta}{\delta x}\left(\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta y}\right) - \frac{\delta}{\delta y}\left(\frac{\delta\phi}{\delta x}\right)\right\}</div>
i^⋅(0)−j^⋅(0)+k^⋅(0)=0⃗\hat{i}\cdot(0) - \hat{j}\cdot(0) + \hat{k}\cdot(0) = \vec{0}
∴\therefore the curl of the gradient of ϕ\phi in the stem will be zero.
