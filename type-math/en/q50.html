Position vectors and 3-D geometry

Question 50

Cumilla Cadet College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 55

A farmer has a plot of land in the shape of a parallelogram whose two adjacent sides are P⃗=4i^−4j^+k^\vec{P} = 4\hat{i} - 4\hat{j} + \hat{k} and Q⃗=2i^−2j^−k^\vec{Q} = 2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} - \hat{k} respectively. On the other hand, a person planned to build a three-dimensional structure using three vectors A⃗=3i^−4j^+5k^\vec{A} = 3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}, B⃗=2i^−j^+k^\vec{B} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k} and C⃗=i^+2j^−3k^\vec{C} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 3\hat{k} respectively.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the area of the farmer's land with the help of the diagonal.

Hint Try it first

Form the two diagonals P+Q and P−Q. Their cross product gives twice the area.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know, The diagonals of a parallelogram are respectively equal to the sum and the difference of its sides. First diagonal,
d1⃗=P⃗+Q⃗\vec{d_1} = \vec{P} + \vec{Q}
or, d1⃗=(4+2)i^+(−4−2)j^+(1−1)k^\text{or, } \vec{d_1} = (4 + 2)\hat{i} + (-4 - 2)\hat{j} + (1 - 1)\hat{k}
∴d1⃗=6i^−6j^\therefore \vec{d_1} = 6\hat{i} - 6\hat{j} Second diagonal, d2⃗=P⃗−Q⃗\vec{d_2} = \vec{P} - \vec{Q}
or, d2⃗=(4−2)i^+(−4+2)j^+(1+1)k^\text{or, } \vec{d_2} = (4 - 2)\hat{i} + (-4 + 2)\hat{j} + (1 + 1)\hat{k}
∴d2⃗=2i^−2j^+2k^\therefore \vec{d_2} = 2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}
or, Here,
Vector product of the two diagonals,
d1⃗×d2⃗=∣i^j^k^6−602−22∣\vec{d_1} \times \vec{d_2} = \left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 6 & -6 & 0 \\ 2 & -2 & 2 \end{matrix}\right|
or, d1⃗×d2⃗=i^(−12−0)−j^(12−0)+k^(−12+12)\text{or, } \vec{d_1} \times \vec{d_2} = \hat{i}(-12 - 0) - \hat{j}(12 - 0) + \hat{k}(-12 + 12)
∴d1⃗×d2⃗=−12i^−12j^+0k^\therefore \vec{d_1} \times \vec{d_2} = -12\hat{i} - 12\hat{j} + 0\hat{k} Magnitude of the vector product, ∣d1⃗×d2⃗∣=(−12)2+(−12)2+02|\vec{d_1} \times \vec{d_2}| = \sqrt{(-12)^2 + (-12)^2 + 0^2}
∴∣d1⃗×d2⃗∣=16.97\therefore |\vec{d_1} \times \vec{d_2}| = 16.97 Area of the parallelogram with the help of the diagonal, A=12∣d1⃗×d2⃗∣A = \frac{1}{2}|\vec{d_1} \times \vec{d_2}|
or, A=12×16.97\text{or, } A = \frac{1}{2} \times 16.97
∴A=8.485 square units Ans.\therefore A = 8.485\text{ square units Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

Will that person's plan succeed or not — analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

A frame with volume needs more than three nonzero edge lengths. Test the scalar triple product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The scalar triple product of the three vectors A⃗\vec{A}, B⃗\vec{B} and C⃗\vec{C},
A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=∣3−452−1112−3∣\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = \left|\begin{matrix} 3 & -4 & 5 \\ 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & -3 \end{matrix}\right|
or, A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=3((−1)(−3)−(1)(2))−((−4)(2)(−3)−(1)(1))+5((2)(2)−(−1)(1))\text{or, } \vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = 3((-1)(-3) - (1)(2)) - ((-4)(2)(-3) - (1)(1)) + 5((2)(2) - (-1)(1))

or, A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=3(3−2)+4(−6−1)+5(4+1)=3(1)+4(−7)+5(5)=28−28\text{or, } \vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = 3(3 - 2) + 4(-6 - 1) + 5(4 + 1) = 3(1) + 4(-7) + 5(5) = 28 - 28
∴A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=0\therefore \vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = 0
or, Here,
A⃗=3i^−4j^+5k^\vec{A} = 3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}
B⃗=2i^−j^+k^\vec{B} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}
C⃗=i^+2j^−3k^\vec{C} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}
Mathematical analysis shows that the value of the scalar triple product of the three vectors is zero. This means that the three vectors lie in the same plane (Coplanar\text{Coplanar}). Since the three vectors lie in the same plane, no three-dimensional structure (such as a parallelepiped) can be made from them, because the volume of the solid formed by them is zero. Therefore, that person's plan will not succeed.
