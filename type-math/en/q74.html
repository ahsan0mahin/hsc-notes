Part c · 3 marks

What is the angle between B⃗\vec{B} and C⃗\vec{C}?

Hint Try it first

Divide B·C by the product of their magnitudes and check the sign before taking arccos.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know, the dot product of two vectors B⃗\vec{B} and C⃗\vec{C} is, B⃗⋅C⃗=∣B⃗∣ ∣C⃗∣cos⁡θ\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = |\vec{B}|\,|\vec{C}|\cos\theta
or, cos⁡θ=B⃗⋅C⃗∣B⃗∣ ∣C⃗∣\text{or, } \cos\theta = \frac{\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C}}{|\vec{B}|\,|\vec{C}|}
or, Here,

now, B⃗⋅C⃗=(i^−3j^+5k^)⋅(2i^+j^−3k^)\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = (\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}) \cdot (2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - 3\hat{k})
or, B⃗⋅C⃗=(1×2)+(−3×1)+(5×−3)\text{or, } \vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = (1 \times 2) + (-3 \times 1) + (5 \times -3)

or, B⃗⋅C⃗=2−3−15\text{or, } \vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = 2 - 3 - 15
∴B⃗⋅C⃗=−16\therefore \vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = -16
∣B⃗∣=12+(−3)2+52|\vec{B}| = \sqrt{1^2 + (-3)^2 + 5^2}

∣B⃗∣=1+9+25|\vec{B}| = \sqrt{1 + 9 + 25}

∣B⃗∣=35|\vec{B}| = \sqrt{35}
∣C⃗∣=22+12+(−3)2|\vec{C}| = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2 + (-3)^2}

∣C⃗∣=4+1+9|\vec{C}| = \sqrt{4 + 1 + 9}

∣C⃗∣=14|\vec{C}| = \sqrt{14}
cos⁡θ=−1635×14\cos\theta = \frac{-16}{\sqrt{35} \times \sqrt{14}}

cos⁡θ=−16490\cos\theta = \frac{-16}{\sqrt{490}}

cos⁡θ=−1622.136\cos\theta = \frac{-16}{22.136}

cos⁡θ=−0.7228\cos\theta = -0.7228
or, θ=cos⁡−1(−0.7228)\text{or, } \theta = \cos^{-1}(-0.7228)

or, θ=136.29∘ Ans.\text{or, } \theta = 136.29^\circ\text{ Ans.}
