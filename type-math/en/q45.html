River–boat and current

Question 45

Mymensingh Girls' Cadet College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 53

In a river 400400 metres wide, the velocity of the current is 2 ms−12\text{ ms}^{-1}. A boat is moving perpendicular to the river with velocity 10 ms−110\text{ ms}^{-1} relative to the water.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine how much time the boat will take to reach the other bank.

Hint Try it first

The perpendicular component is already known; the current acts along the shore.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know,
t=dusin⁡αt = \frac{d}{u\sin\alpha}
or, t=40010sin⁡90∘\text{or, } t = \frac{400}{10\sin 90^\circ}
or, t=40010×1\text{or, } t = \frac{400}{10 \times 1}
or, t=40\text{or, } t = 40
∴t=40 s Ans.\therefore t = 40\text{ s Ans.}

Part d · 4 marks

Two vectors AA and ACAC respectively, and a vector field θ\theta.

Hint Try it first

Calculate drift over the time found in (c). Combine drift and width as perpendicular displacements.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Even though the boat set off along the width of the river, because of the current it will drift some distance away and reach point CC. Displacement along the bank of the river,
x=v×tx = v \times t
or, x=2×40\text{or, } x = 2 \times 40
∴x=80 m\therefore x = 80\text{ m}
Total displacement of the boat (ACAC), ss: Since the width of the river (dd) and the horizontal displacement (xx) are mutually perpendicular, According to Pythagoras' theorem, s=d2+x2s = \sqrt{d^2 + x^2}
or, s=(400)2+(80)2\text{or, } s = \sqrt{(400)^2 + (80)^2}
∴s=407.921 m\therefore s = 407.921\text{ m}
tan⁡θ=width of the riverhorizontal displacement\tan\theta = \frac{\text{width of the river}}{\text{horizontal displacement}}

tan⁡θ=dx\tan\theta = \frac{d}{x}
or, tan⁡θ=40080\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{400}{80}
or, tan⁡θ=5\text{or, } \tan\theta = 5
or, θ=tan⁡−1(5)\text{or, } \theta = \tan^{-1}(5)
∴θ=78.690∘\therefore \theta = 78.690^\circ
or, Direction of displacement (θ\theta): let the resultant displacement ss make an angle θ\theta with the bank of the river.
Therefore, the boat will reach the other bank at a distance 407.921 m407.921\text{ m} from the starting point (AA) and at an angle 78.690∘78.690^\circ with the river bank.
