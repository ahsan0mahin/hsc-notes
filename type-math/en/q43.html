River–boat and current

Question 43

Dhaka Board · 2015 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 52

In the figure, the breadth of the flowing river is 1.5 km1.5\text{ km} and the velocity of the current is 4 kmh−14\text{ kmh}^{-1}. Boatman Rahmat rows along ABAB and reaches the opposite bank along ACAC. The velocity of the boat is 3 kmh−13\text{ kmh}^{-1}.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the distance travelled by the boat along ACAC.

Hint Try it first

Compute AC once using the given angle and once using the speeds. Do the source data agree?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Alternative method, Velocity of the current, u=4 km⋅h−1u = 4\text{ km}\cdot\text{h}^{-1} Velocity of the boat, v=3 km⋅h−1v = 3\text{ km}\cdot\text{h}^{-1} Time required for the boat to cross, t=ABv=1.5 km3 km⋅h−1=0.5 ht = \frac{AB}{v} = \frac{1.5\text{ km}}{3\text{ km}\cdot\text{h}^{-1}} = 0.5\text{ h} At this time the boat will cover a distance BCBC in the direction of the current, so
∴BC=u×t=4 km⋅h−1×0.5 h=2 km\therefore BC = u \times t = 4\text{ km}\cdot\text{h}^{-1} \times 0.5\text{ h} = 2\text{ km} Therefore AC2=AB2+BC2=(1.5 km)2+(2 km)2=6.25 km2AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 = (1.5\text{ km})^2 + (2\text{ km})^2 = 6.25\text{ km}^2
∴AC=2.5 km\therefore AC = 2.5\text{ km}
(Two different values of ACAC are obtained by the two methods. Hence the given information is erroneous.)

Part d · 4 marks

Can boatman Rahmat reach point BB by rowing the boat along ADAD? Give your opinion with mathematical analysis.

Hint Try it first

For AD the upstream component must exactly cancel the river current.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

If the boat is rowed along ADAD, according to the figure the angle between the velocity of the current uu and the velocity of the boat vv is α=180∘−30∘=150∘\alpha = 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ. Therefore, if the angle between the velocity of the current and the resultant velocity is θ\theta, then
tan⁡θ=vsin⁡αu+vcos⁡α\tan\theta = \frac{v\sin\alpha}{u + v\cos\alpha}

tan⁡θ=3sin⁡150∘4+3cos⁡150∘\tan\theta = \frac{3\sin 150^\circ}{4 + 3\cos 150^\circ}

tan⁡θ=3×0.54+3×(−0.866025)\tan\theta = \frac{3 \times 0.5}{4 + 3 \times (-0.866025)}

tan⁡θ=1.54−2.598\tan\theta = \frac{1.5}{4 - 2.598}

tan⁡θ=1.07\tan\theta = 1.07
∴θ=46.91∘\therefore \theta = 46.91^\circ Here, θ<90∘\theta < 90^\circ. Therefore the boat will not be able to cross the river along ABAB.

Alternative answer: according to the figure, the angle between the velocity of the current uu and the velocity of the boat vv is α=180∘−30∘=150∘\alpha = 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ. Therefore, if the angle between the velocity of the current and the resultant velocity is θ\theta, then
tan⁡θ=vsin⁡αu+vcos⁡α\tan\theta = \frac{v\sin\alpha}{u + v\cos\alpha} Now, for θ=90∘\theta = 90^\circ to happen u+vcos⁡α=0u + v\cos\alpha = 0 must hold. But
u+vcos⁡α=4+3cos⁡150∘u + v\cos\alpha = 4 + 3\cos 150^\circ

u+vcos⁡α=4+3×(−0.866025)u + v\cos\alpha = 4 + 3 \times (-0.866025)

u+vcos⁡α=4−2.598u + v\cos\alpha = 4 - 2.598
