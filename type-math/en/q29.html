If the magnitudes of P⃗\vec{P} and Q⃗\vec{Q} represent the speeds of a boat and of the current in a river respectively, then the boat takes 22 minutes to cross the river along the shortest path.

If the boatman wants to cross the river in the minimum time, will he cover a distance greater than the width of the river — explain with the help of mathematical values.

Hint Try it first

Find the width from the direct-crossing time. Compare the diagonal path on the fastest crossing with that width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

SD=38+1838\frac{S}{D}=\frac{\sqrt{38+18}}{\sqrt{38}}

The speeds are √38 and √18. The direct crossing speed is √20; the minimum-time path-to-width ratio is √56/√38≈1.214. The route is longer.
