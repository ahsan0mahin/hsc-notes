Hint Try it first

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The perpendicular projection of the vector A⃗\vec{A} along the vector B⃗\vec{B} is—
Acos⁡θ=A⃗⋅B⃗BA\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{B}
