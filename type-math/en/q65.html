Part c · 3 marks

Determine the angle between P⃗\vec{P} and the positive xx-axis.

Hint Try it first

Compare the x component with the full magnitude of P.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡θx=2/3\cos\theta_x=2/3

The direction cosine is Px/∥P∥=2/3P_x/\lVert P\rVert=2/3, so the angle is about 48.19°.
