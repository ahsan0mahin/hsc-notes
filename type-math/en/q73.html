Question 73

Cumilla Government Women's College · Board question no. 1 · book p. 67

In a river of width 15 km15\text{ km} the velocity of the current is 5 km/h5\text{ km/h}. The 1st boatman sets off with velocity 10 km/h10\text{ km/h} at an angle α\alpha with the current, and the 2nd boatman with velocity 10 km/h10\text{ km/h} at an angle 90∘90^\circ with the current.

Part c · 3 marks

For what value of the angle α\alpha will the 1st boatman reach straight across the other bank?

Hint Try it first

Be clear whether α is measured from the bank or from the perpendicular before using cosine.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡α=−5/10\cos\alpha=-5/10

10 cos α+5=0 gives 120° from downstream, or 30° upstream from the perpendicular.

Part d · 4 marks

Which boatman will reach the other bank first? Explain through mathematical analysis.

Hint Try it first

Compare only the components normal to the banks for the same 15 km width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t1=15/(10sin⁡120∘),t2=15/10t_1=15/(10\sin120^\circ),\quad t_2=15/10

The first takes 15/(10 sin120°)≈1.73 h; the second takes 15/10=1.5 h and arrives first.
