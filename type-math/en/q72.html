River–boat and current

Question 72

City Residential Model College, Dinajpur · Board question no. 8 · book p. 67

In a river of width 4.5 km4.5\text{ km} the velocity of the current is 2.5 kmh−12.5\text{ kmh}^{-1}. In a swimming competition to cross the river, the first swimmer swims with velocity 5 kmh−15\text{ kmh}^{-1} at an angle 60∘60^\circ with the upstream direction, and the second swimmer swims with velocity 4.4 kmh−14.4\text{ kmh}^{-1} at an angle 79.8∘79.8^\circ with the current.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the resultant velocity of the first swimmer.

Hint Try it first

Find the across-bank component of the first swimmer and check the net downstream component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=5sin⁡120∘R=5\sin120^\circ

Downstream component is 2.5−5 cos60°=0. Across speed is 5 sin60°≈4.33 km/h.

Part d · 4 marks

Discuss the result of the said competition with mathematical analysis.

Hint Try it first

Retain full precision until both crossing times are expressed in the same units.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Δt=3600(4.55sin⁡120∘−4.54.4sin⁡79.8∘) s\Delta t=3600\left(\frac{4.5}{5\sin120^\circ}-\frac{4.5}{4.4\sin79.8^\circ}\right)\,\mathrm s

Times are 4.5/(5 sin60°)≈1.03923 h and 4.5/(4.4 sin79.8°)≈1.03915 h. Nearly tied; at the stated precision the second is about 0.29 s earlier.
