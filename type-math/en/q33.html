Question 33

Chattogram Board · 2023 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 46

In the figure, ABAB is a road running along the bank of the river BCBC. The velocity of the current in the river is 2 ms−12\text{ ms}^{-1} and a boatman is rowing a boat with velocity 4 ms−14\text{ ms}^{-1}. The velocity of a car on the road is 15 ms−115\text{ ms}^{-1}. The car makes a stop of 40 s40\text{ s} at station BB.

Part c · 3 marks

In the minimum time, how long can the boatman cross the river?

Hint Try it first

What heading gives the greatest possible component across BC?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

To cross the river in the minimum time, the boatman must set off at right angles to the current, towards the point exactly opposite on the other bank.
t=dvsin⁡θt = \frac{d}{v\sin\theta}
or, t=0.5×10004sin⁡90∘\text{or, } t = \frac{0.5 \times 1000}{4\sin 90^\circ}
or, t=5004\text{or, } t = \frac{500}{4}
or, t=125 s\text{or, } t = 125\text{ s}
∴t=2 minutes 5 seconds\therefore t = 2\text{ minutes } 5\text{ seconds}
∴\therefore the boatman will be able to cross the river in the least time of 22 minutes 55 seconds. Ans.
