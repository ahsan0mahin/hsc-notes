River–boat and current

Question 41

Sylhet Board · 2021 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 50

From point 'A' on the bank of a river of width 4 km4\text{ km}, a boat set off with velocity 12 km/hr12\text{ km/hr} to cross to the other bank. A man waited at point 'B' on the opposite bank for 2020 minutes from the start of the boat and then left. The velocity of the current in the river is 6 kmh−16\text{ kmh}^{-1}.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the magnitude of the resultant velocity of the boat.

Hint Try it first

Treat aiming squarely across as the initial condition for this calculation.

Part d · 4 marks

Will the boat meet the waiting man in the stem? Verify mathematically.

Hint Try it first

For a meeting at B, first change the heading to cancel drift. Then compare the arrival time with the deadline.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

If the boat, setting off from point A, reaches point B on the other bank within 20 minutes, it will meet the waiting man. For this, if the boat is rowed along the width of the river, then instead of reaching point B it will reach the other bank to the right of B. Let the boat, rowed at an angle α\alpha with the current, reach point B, and let the direction of the resultant be along the width of the river, i.e. at an angle of 90° with the velocity of the current.
Here,

The angle between P⃗\vec P and Q⃗\vec Q is α\alpha and the angle between R⃗\vec R and P⃗\vec P, θ=90∘\theta=90^\circ.
We know,

tan⁡θ=Qsin⁡αP+Qcos⁡α\tan\theta=\frac{Q\sin\alpha}{P+Q\cos\alpha}
or,

tan⁡90∘=10\tan90^\circ=\frac10

tan⁡90∘=Qsin⁡αP+Qcos⁡α\tan90^\circ = \frac{Q\sin\alpha}{P+Q\cos\alpha}
or, P+Qcos⁡α=0P+Q\cos\alpha=0 or, cos⁡α=−P/Q\cos\alpha=-P/Q
or,

α=cos⁡−1(−612)\alpha=\cos^{-1}\left(-\frac6{12}\right)

α=cos⁡−1(−12)\alpha = \cos^{-1}\left(-\frac12\right)

α=120∘.\alpha = 120^\circ.
∴ resultant,

<div class="math">R=P2+Q2+2PQcos⁡α=62+122+2×6×12×(−12)=180−72=63 km h−1.\begin{aligned} R&=\sqrt{P^2+Q^2+2PQ\cos\alpha}\\ &=\sqrt{6^2+12^2+2\times6\times12\times\left(-\frac12\right)}\\ &=\sqrt{180-72}=6\sqrt3\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}. \end{aligned}</div>
Here, if the boat takes time t hour to go to point B on the other bank,

t=dRt=\frac dR

t=463 ht = \frac4{6\sqrt3}\ \mathrm h

t=0.385 ht = 0.385\ \mathrm h

t=23.1 min>20 min.t = 23.1\ \mathrm{min}>20\ \mathrm{min}.
Mathematical analysis shows that the man waiting at point B will leave long before the time the boat takes to reach the other bank, so the boat will not meet him.
