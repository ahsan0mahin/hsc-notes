Part d · 4 marks

Will the triangle in the stem be a right-angled triangle — analyse mathematically.

Hint Try it first

At a right angle the adjacent sides have zero dot product. Test all three pairs.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

If the triangle in the stem is a right-angled triangle, the sum of the squares of two of its sides will be equal to the square of the other side (the hypotenuse). The angle between them must be a right angle, i.e. the dot product of two of the sides (other than the hypotenuse) must be zero.
A⃗⋅B⃗=(2i^−3j^+k^)⋅(3i^−j^+5k^)\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = (2\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}) \cdot (3\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 5\hat{k})

A⃗⋅B⃗=6+3+5\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 6 + 3 + 5

A⃗⋅B⃗=14≠0\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 14 \neq 0
A⃗⋅C⃗=(2i^−3j^+k^)⋅(3i^+2j^−4k^)\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C} = (2\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}) \cdot (3\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 4\hat{k})

A⃗⋅C⃗=6−6−4\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C} = 6 - 6 - 4

A⃗⋅C⃗=−4≠0\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C} = -4 \neq 0
B⃗⋅C⃗=(3i^−j^+5k^)⋅(3i^+2j^−4k^)\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = (3\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 5\hat{k}) \cdot (3\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 4\hat{k})

B⃗⋅C⃗=9−2−20\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = 9 - 2 - 20

B⃗⋅C⃗=−13≠0\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C} = -13 \neq 0

∴\therefore Therefore it can be said that ΔABC\Delta ABC will not be a right-angled triangle.
