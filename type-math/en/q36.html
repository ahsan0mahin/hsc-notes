Force, torque and rotation

Question 36

Barishal Board · 2023 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 48

Nahin and Jahin were dragging a heavy stationary truck by tying two ropes to it. An angle of 'α\alpha' is created between the two ropes. The truck was moving towards Nahin. Nahin told Jahin to apply more force.

Part c · 3 marks

Show that if Jahin applies a force equal in magnitude to Nahin's, their resultant force will bisect the angle between them.

Hint Try it first

Construct the parallelogram of the equal pulls and compare the triangles on either side of the diagonal.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Let the forces applied by Jahin and Nahin be PP and QQ respectively, the angle between the two forces α\alpha and the resultant RR. If the resultant RR acts at an angle θ\theta with PP, then
tan⁡θ=Qsin⁡αP+Qcos⁡α\tan\theta = \frac{Q\sin\alpha}{P + Q\cos\alpha} If Jahin applies a force equal to Nahin's then P=QP = Q
∴tan⁡θ=Qsin⁡αQ+Qcos⁡α\therefore \tan\theta = \frac{Q\sin\alpha}{Q + Q\cos\alpha}
or, tan⁡θ=Qsin⁡αQ(1+cos⁡α)\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{Q\sin\alpha}{Q(1 + \cos\alpha)}

or, tan⁡θ=sin⁡α1+cos⁡α\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{\sin\alpha}{1 + \cos\alpha}

or, tan⁡θ=2sin⁡α2cos⁡α22cos⁡2α2\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}{2\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2}}

or, tan⁡θ=sin⁡α2cos⁡α2\text{or, } \tan\theta = \frac{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}
∴tan⁡θ=tan⁡α2\therefore \tan\theta = \tan\frac{\alpha}{2}
θ=α2\theta = \frac{\alpha}{2} Therefore, their resultant force will bisect the angle between them. (Shown)
