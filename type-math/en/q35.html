Question 35

Jashore Board · 2023 · Board question no. 1 · book p. 47

A⃗=i^+j^−k^\vec{A} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k} and B⃗=2i^−2j^−3k^\vec{B} = 2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} - 3\hat{k} are two vectors.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the area of the quadrilateral formed by the two vectors described in the stem.

Hint Try it first

Treat the vectors as adjacent sides, not diagonals.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Let the area of the quadrilateral formed by the two vectors A⃗\vec{A} and B⃗\vec{B} be Δ\Delta Given, Δ=∣A⃗×B⃗∣\Delta = |\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| Now,
A⃗×B⃗=∣i^j^k^11−12−2−3∣\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \left|\begin{matrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 2 & -2 & -3 \end{matrix}\right|
or, A⃗×B⃗=(−3−2)i^−(−3+2)j^+(−2−2)k^\text{or, } \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = (-3 - 2)\hat{i} - (-3 + 2)\hat{j} + (-2 - 2)\hat{k}
or, A⃗×B⃗=−5i^+j^−4k^\text{or, } \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = -5\hat{i} + \hat{j} - 4\hat{k}
∴\therefore area of the quadrilateral, Δ=∣A⃗×B⃗∣=(−5)2+12+(−4)2=42 square units Ans.\Delta = |\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = \sqrt{(-5)^2 + 1^2 + (-4)^2} = \sqrt{42}\text{ square units Ans.}
or, Here,
A⃗=i^+j^−k^\vec{A} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}
B⃗=2i^−2j^−3k^\vec{B} = 2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}

Part d · 4 marks

Verify mathematically whether the two vectors are mutually perpendicular.

Hint Try it first

Test the sign and magnitude of their dot product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

We know, if two vectors A⃗\vec A and B⃗\vec B are mutually perpendicular, their scalar product will be zero.
that is, A⃗⋅B⃗=0\vec A\cdot\vec B=0.
Now,

<div class="math">A⃗⋅B⃗=(i^+j^−k^)⋅(2i^−2j^−3k^)=(1×2)+1×(−2)+(−1)×(−3)=2−2+3=3.\begin{aligned} \vec A\cdot\vec B&=(\hat i+\hat j-\hat k)\cdot(2\hat i-2\hat j-3\hat k)\\ &=(1\times2)+1\times(-2)+(-1)\times(-3)\\ &=2-2+3\\ &=3. \end{aligned}</div>
Here, A⃗=i^+j^−k^\vec A=\hat i+\hat j-\hat k; B⃗=2i^−2j^−3k^\vec B=2\hat i-2\hat j-3\hat k.
Since A⃗⋅B⃗≠0\vec A\cdot\vec B\ne0, therefore it can be said that the two vectors are not mutually perpendicular.
In (c) 'quadrilateral' means the parallelogram formed by taking the two vectors as adjacent sides. In (b) the equality of the magnitudes of the vectors is explained; as vectors, if A⃗+B⃗=A⃗−B⃗\vec A+\vec B=\vec A-\vec B then B⃗=0⃗\vec B=\vec0 must hold.
