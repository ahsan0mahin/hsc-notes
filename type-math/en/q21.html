River–boat and current

Question 21

Cumilla Board · 2024 · Board question no. 5 · book p. 41

A bus is moving with velocity 4ms−14\text{ms}^{-1} along the bank of a river of width 1 km1\text{ km}. Seeing the bus straight across from the other bank, a person sets off by boat with velocity 6 ms−16\text{ ms}^{-1} at an angle 70∘70^\circ with the current in order to catch the bus. The velocity of the current in the river is 2ms−12\text{ms}^{-1}.

Part c · 3 marks

What was the resultant velocity of the boat?
