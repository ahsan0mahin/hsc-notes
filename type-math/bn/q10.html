Acos⁡θ=A⃗⋅B⃗BA\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{B}
Acos⁡θ=(2i^−2j^+2k^)⋅(i^−3j^+6k^)12+(−3)2+62A\cos\theta = \frac{(2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}) \cdot (\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 6\hat{k})}{\sqrt{1^2 + (-3)^2 + 6^2}}
Acos⁡θ=2046=2.949 Ans.A\cos\theta = \frac{20}{\sqrt{46}} = 2.949\text{ Ans.}

এখানে, cos⁡θ1=A⃗⋅B⃗AB\cos\theta_1 = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{AB}

∴θ1=cos⁡−1(0.851)\therefore \theta_1 = \cos^{-1}(0.851)
