A⃗\vec{A} এর ডাইভারজেন্স div A⃗\text{div}\,\vec{A}

∇⃗⋅A⃗=∂∂x(2x+y)+∂∂y(3y+z2)+∂∂z(−5z+x)\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{A} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(2x + y) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(3y + z^2) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(-5z + x)
∇⃗⋅A⃗=2+3−5=0\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{A} = 2 + 3 - 5 = 0 যেহেতু ∇⃗⋅A⃗=0\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{A} = 0, তাই A⃗\vec{A} ভেক্টরটি সলিনয়ডাল।

i^{∂∂y(−5z+x)−∂∂z(3y+z2)}−j^{∂∂x(−5z+x)−∂∂z(2x+y)}+k^{∂∂x(3y+z2)−∂∂y(2x+y)}\hat{i}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(-5z + x) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(3y + z^2)\right\} - \hat{j}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(-5z + x) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(2x + y)\right\} + \hat{k}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(3y + z^2) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(2x + y)\right\}

B⃗\vec{B} এর ডাইভারজেন্স div B⃗\text{div}\,\vec{B}
