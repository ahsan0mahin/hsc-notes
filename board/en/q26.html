Question and stimulus

Two swimmers cross a 20 km wide river with an 8 km/h current. Both swim at 16 km/h. The first aims 10° upstream of AB (the width); the second aims 60° to the current.

  1. (a)

    What are Cartesian unit vectors?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why is pulling a lawn roller easier than pushing it?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    The first swimmer aims along AC but lands at D. Find BD.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Which swimmer crosses first?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

AB spans the 20 km width. The first swimmer points 10° upstream of AB and drifts to D; the second points 60° to the downstream bank. Take care which axis defines each angle.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What are Cartesian unit vectors?

Hint Try it first

Look along the positive coordinate axes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They are three mutually perpendicular vectors of unit magnitude pointing along positive x, y and z.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is pulling a lawn roller easier than pushing it?

Hint Try it first

Compare the vertical components of the applied forces.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

An upward pull reduces the normal reaction; a downward push increases it. With resistance increasing with the load, pushing is harder.

Part c · 3 marks

The first swimmer aims along AC but lands at D. Find BD.

Hint Try it first

The 10° angle is measured from the width. Decide which component uses sine and which uses cosine.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

BD=(8−16sin⁡10∘)2016cos⁡10∘ kmBD=(8-16\sin10^\circ)\frac{20}{16\cos10^\circ}\,\mathrm{km}

Time is 20/(16 cos10°) h. Downstream speed is 8−16 sin10°; multiplying gives about 6.63 km.

Part d · 4 marks

Which swimmer crosses first?

Hint Try it first

The second swimmer’s 60° is measured from downstream, not from the width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t1=2016cos⁡10∘<t2=2016sin⁡60∘t_1=\frac{20}{16\cos10^\circ}<t_2=\frac{20}{16\sin60^\circ}

The times are 1.27 h and 20/(16 sin60°)≈1.44 h respectively. The first swimmer arrives earlier.
