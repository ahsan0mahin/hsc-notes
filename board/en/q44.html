Question and stimulus

OACB is a parallelogram: OA=P, OB=Q, angle AOB=30°, with side lengths 5 and 3.

  1. (a)

    Define collinear vectors.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Explain the physical significance of divergence.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the magnitude of P−Q.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Verify that the areas of triangles AOC and BOC sum to the parallelogram’s area.

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

OA and OB are the adjacent sides. OC is their resultant and divides the parallelogram into triangles AOC and BOC.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define collinear vectors.

Hint Try it first

Compare lines of action, not lengths.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They lie along the same line or parallel lines; their senses may agree or oppose each other.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain the physical significance of divergence.

Hint Try it first

Compare flow into and out of a small volume.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For a velocity field positive divergence indicates net local outflow, negative divergence net inflow, and zero divergence no net volume flux per unit volume.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the magnitude of P−Q.

Hint Try it first

Use the angle between P and Q in the cosine rule for their difference.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∣P⃗−Q⃗∣=25+9−30cos⁡30∘|\vec P-\vec Q|=\sqrt{25+9-30\cos30^\circ}

The magnitude is √(25+9−30 cos30°)≈2.83.

Part d · 4 marks

Verify that the areas of triangles AOC and BOC sum to the parallelogram’s area.

Hint Try it first

What does a diagonal do to the area of a parallelogram? Verify using base and height.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

[AOC]=[BOC]=12(5)(3)sin⁡30∘[AOC]=[BOC]=\tfrac12(5)(3)\sin30^\circ

Area=5×3×sin30°=7.5. Diagonal OC divides it into equal triangles of 3.75 square units each.

Source notes and corrections

The squared magnitude contains 25+9, not 25−9. Area uses a cross product, not a vector sum.
