Question and stimulus

Two swimmers race across a 4.5 km wide river flowing at 2.5 km/h. The first swims at 5 km/h at 60° to upstream; the second at 4.4 km/h at 79.8° to downstream.

  1. (a)

    Define the gradient of a scalar field.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    What does 50% relative humidity mean?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the first swimmer’s resultant velocity.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Compare the swimmers’ crossing times and decide the result.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the gradient of a scalar field.

Hint Try it first

Compare rates of change in different directions.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Its components are the three spatial partial derivatives. It points in the direction of steepest increase; its magnitude is that maximum rate.

Part b · 2 marks

What does 50% relative humidity mean?

Hint Try it first

Compare actual and saturation vapour pressures at the same temperature.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The actual water-vapour pressure is half the saturation pressure at the air temperature.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the first swimmer’s resultant velocity.

Hint Try it first

Find the across-bank component of the first swimmer and check the net downstream component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=5sin⁡120∘R=5\sin120^\circ

Downstream component is 2.5−5 cos60°=0. Across speed is 5 sin60°≈4.33 km/h.

Part d · 4 marks

Compare the swimmers’ crossing times and decide the result.

Hint Try it first

Retain full precision until both crossing times are expressed in the same units.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Δt=3600(4.55sin⁡120∘−4.54.4sin⁡79.8∘) s\Delta t=3600\left(\frac{4.5}{5\sin120^\circ}-\frac{4.5}{4.4\sin79.8^\circ}\right)\,\mathrm s

Times are 4.5/(5 sin60°)≈1.03923 h and 4.5/(4.4 sin79.8°)≈1.03915 h. Nearly tied; at the stated precision the second is about 0.29 s earlier.

Source notes and corrections

Do not round the times before comparing them: the difference from the given values is only about 0.29 s, smaller than the precision suggested by the inputs.
