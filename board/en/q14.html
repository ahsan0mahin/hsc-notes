The perpendicular vectors v=(2,3,−x) and u=(3,2,2) have magnitudes equal to the boat and current speeds respectively. Crossing by the shortest path takes 4 min.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

When is the resultant of three vectors zero?

Hint Try it first

Trace all three in order.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They sum to zero when placed head to tail in a closed triangle, so the final head returns to the starting point.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine x.

Hint Try it first

The two vectors are perpendicular. Which product gives an equation in x?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

v⃗⋅u⃗=12−2x=0\vec v\cdot\vec u=12-2x=0

Their dot product is 6+6−2x=0; hence x=6.

Part d · 4 marks

For a minimum-time crossing must the boat cover more than the river width? Verify.

Hint Try it first

Draw the minimum-path and minimum-time headings separately; the current affects their ground tracks differently.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

S=D49+177>DS=D\frac{\sqrt{49+17}}{7}>D

The speeds are 7 and √17. On the shortest path the crossing component is √(49−17). On the fastest crossing it is 7, but the ground speed is √66. The path-to-width ratio √66/7 exceeds one.

Source notes and corrections

Only the magnitudes of the given perpendicular vectors are subsequently used as speeds. Distances in metres require the additional assumption that those speeds are in m/s.
