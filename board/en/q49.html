Question and stimulus

Soumik sees rain fall vertically at 6 km/h. A pedestrian walks at 4 km/h and a cyclist travels at 8 km/h, holding their umbrellas at different angles.

  1. (a)

    Define relative humidity.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Can an object moving at constant speed around a circle accelerate?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    At what angle should the pedestrian hold the umbrella?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Verify that the cyclist needs a different angle.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define relative humidity.

Hint Try it first

Compare actual vapour pressure with saturation at the same temperature.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the actual water-vapour pressure divided by the saturation vapour pressure at that temperature, expressed as a percentage.

Part b · 2 marks

Can an object moving at constant speed around a circle accelerate?

Hint Try it first

Compare successive velocity arrows.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Yes. Even at fixed speed the direction changes continuously, giving inward acceleration v2/rv^2/r.

Part c · 3 marks

At what angle should the pedestrian hold the umbrella?

Hint Try it first

In the pedestrian’s frame the horizontal rain component has the opposite sign to walking velocity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tan⁡θw=4/6\tan\theta_w=4/6

Relative rain has a backward component of 4 and a downward component of 6 km/h. Tilt forwards by arctan(4/6)≈33.69° from vertical.

Part d · 4 marks

Verify that the cyclist needs a different angle.

Hint Try it first

Repeat the component ratio with the cyclist’s speed, keeping the same vertical component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tan⁡θc=8/6\tan\theta_c=8/6

The cyclist must tilt by arctan(8/6)≈53.13°. The angles differ because the horizontal relative speeds differ.

Source notes and corrections

Both umbrella angles are measured forwards from the vertical. Relative humidity expressed as a percentage includes a factor of 100.
