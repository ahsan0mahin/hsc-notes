Question and stimulus

A boat crosses a 360 m wide river from A and reaches C. Boat speed is 10 m/s, current speed 3 m/s. The diagram marks 37° to the bank; B is directly opposite A.

  1. (a)

    Define curl.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Can an object have constant velocity on a circular path?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Calculate BC along the far bank.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    How should the boatman steer to reach B?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

A and B are directly opposite; BC is the distance along the far bank. The printed solution reads 37° as the heading of the boat relative to water.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define curl.

Hint Try it first

Imagine a tiny paddle wheel in the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Curl, ∇×V⃗\nabla\times\vec V, measures local circulation per unit area. Its direction is the local axis of rotation given by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

Can an object have constant velocity on a circular path?

Hint Try it first

Compare velocity at two different points.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

No. Speed may be constant, but the tangent direction continually changes, so the velocity changes.

Part c · 3 marks

Calculate BC along the far bank.

Hint Try it first

Use the 37° heading to get crossing time. Does the current contribute to BC or to the width?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

BC=360(3+10cos⁡37∘)10sin⁡37∘BC=\frac{360(3+10\cos37^\circ)}{10\sin37^\circ}

Interpreting 37° as the heading relative to water, time=360/(10 sin37°) s. Multiply by 3+10 cos37° to get BC≈657.2 m.

Part d · 4 marks

How should the boatman steer to reach B?

Hint Try it first

Set the net downstream component to zero, preserving the sign of the upstream term.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

3+10cos⁡α=03+10\cos\alpha=0

To cancel the current choose α with 10 cos α=−3: α≈107.46° from downstream.

Source notes and corrections

The diagram can also be read as a ground-track angle. The printed numerical result assumes 10 m/s and 37° refer to motion relative to water.
