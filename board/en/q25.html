Question and stimulus

Two forces act at O. F₁ is 15 N at 30° above the negative x-axis; F₂ is 10 N at 30° above the positive x-axis.

  1. (a)

    What are Cartesian unit vectors?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    How can a vector quantity be converted to a scalar?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Express F₁ as a vector.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Are F₁+F₂ and F₁−F₂ perpendicular?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

Both forces start at O. The 10 N force is 30° above +x; the 15 N force is 30° above −x. Their horizontal components have opposite signs.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What are Cartesian unit vectors?

Hint Try it first

Look along the positive coordinate axes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They are three mutually perpendicular vectors of unit magnitude pointing along positive x, y and z.

Part b · 2 marks

How can a vector quantity be converted to a scalar?

Hint Try it first

Think of measuring its component along a reference direction.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Dotting it with a unit vector gives a signed scalar projection. For a vector field its divergence is also a scalar field.

Part c · 3 marks

Express F₁ as a vector.

Hint Try it first

F₁ is in quadrant II. Which component must be negative?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

F⃗1=15(cos⁡150∘i^+sin⁡150∘j^) N\vec F_1=15(\cos150^\circ\hat i+\sin150^\circ\hat j)\,\mathrm N

From +x the angle is 150°. The components are (−153/2,15/2)(−15\sqrt3/2,15/2) N.

Part d · 4 marks

Are F₁+F₂ and F₁−F₂ perpendicular?

Hint Try it first

Expand the dot product of the sum and difference; the mixed products cancel.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(F⃗1+F⃗2)⋅(F⃗1−F⃗2)=225−100(\vec F_1+\vec F_2)\cdot(\vec F_1-\vec F_2)=225-100

The dot product of the sum and difference is 15²−10²=125 N², not zero.

Source notes and corrections

The force components have units of N. The dot product in (d) has units of N².
