Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Can two vectors have equal sum and difference?

Hint Try it first

Distinguish vector equality from equal magnitudes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Vector equality A+B=A−B requires B=0. Equal magnitudes instead require A·B=0, as follows by squaring both magnitudes.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the area of the quadrilateral formed by A and B as adjacent sides.

Hint Try it first

Treat the vectors as adjacent sides, not diagonals.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∣A⃗×B⃗∣=25+1+16|\vec A\times\vec B|=\sqrt{25+1+16}

The parallelogram area is the length of A×B=(−5,1,−4), namely √42 square units.

Part d · 4 marks

Are the vectors perpendicular?

Hint Try it first

Test the sign and magnitude of their dot product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A⃗⋅B⃗=2−2+3\vec A\cdot\vec B=2-2+3

A·B=2−2+3=3, so they are not perpendicular.

Source notes and corrections

The quadrilateral is the parallelogram spanned by the two given adjacent sides. Equal sum and difference as vectors is a stronger condition than equal lengths.
