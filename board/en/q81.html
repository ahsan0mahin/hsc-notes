Question and stimulus

A bus, jeep and truck have velocities (2,1,−1), (3,−2,4) and (1,−3,5) respectively. They collide at one instant.

  1. (a)

    What is a physical law?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Which coordinate system can describe a butterfly moving around your room?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Determine the curl of the truck velocity.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Verify that the velocities at collision are coplanar.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is a physical law?

Hint Try it first

Distinguish a repeatable relationship from one observation.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A law expresses a consistent relationship between physical quantities under specified conditions.

Part b · 2 marks

Which coordinate system can describe a butterfly moving around your room?

Hint Try it first

Count the independent directions of motion.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Use three Cartesian coordinates relative to a fixed corner or other origin. Two coordinates cannot describe changes in height as well as horizontal motion.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the curl of the truck velocity.

Hint Try it first

The given velocity components are constants. Consider their derivatives with respect to position.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∇×(1,−3,5)=0⃗\nabla\times(1,-3,5)=\vec0

The truck velocity has constant components, so all spatial derivatives and its curl are zero.

Part d · 4 marks

Verify that the velocities at collision are coplanar.

Hint Try it first

Compute the scalar triple product; a vanishing value tests coplanarity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(2,−11,−7)⋅(1,−3,5)=0(2,-11,-7)\cdot(1,-3,5)=0

The cross product of the first two velocities is (2,−11,−7). Dotting with the third gives 2+33−35=0, proving coplanarity.

Source notes and corrections

Curl is computed by interpreting the given constant truck velocity as a field constant throughout space.
