Question and stimulus

A motorboat travels at 15 km/h downstream and 5 km/h upstream. It starts at A to reach B directly opposite on a 5 km wide river. At the same instant a person starts walking towards B from C, 5 km away, at 4 km/h.

  1. (a)

    Define the position (radius) vector.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Explain why k^×j^\hat k\times\hat j is a unit vector.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    At what angle to the current must the boat start?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Will the boatman meet the walker on reaching B? Calculate their arrival times.

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

A and B are on opposite banks. The walker moves from C towards B along the far bank; the boat must arrive at B, not merely somewhere on that bank.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain why k^×j^\hat k\times\hat j is a unit vector.

Hint Try it first

Consider the order of the axes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The factors are perpendicular unit vectors, so the magnitude is one; reversing cyclic order gives −i^-\hat i.

Part c · 3 marks

At what angle to the current must the boat start?

Hint Try it first

Add and subtract the two speeds to separate the boat and current. Which component must vanish for a direct landing?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u=15+52,v=15−52,ucos⁡α+v=0u=\frac{15+5}{2},\quad v=\frac{15-5}{2},\quad u\cos\alpha+v=0

Still-water speed is (15+5)/2=10 km/h; current speed is 5 km/h. To cancel drift, 10cos⁡α+5=010\cos\alpha+5=0, giving 120°.

Part d · 4 marks

Will the boatman meet the walker on reaching B? Calculate their arrival times.

Hint Try it first

Compare time across the width using the remaining component with the time to walk CB.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tB=510sin⁡120∘ h,tC=54 ht_B=\frac{5}{10\sin120^\circ}\,\mathrm h,\quad t_C=\frac54\,\mathrm h

Crossing speed is 10sin⁡120∘=5310\sin120^\circ=5\sqrt3 km/h. The boat takes about 34.6 min; the walker takes 75 min. They do not arrive together. The walker arrives about 40.36 minutes later.

Source notes and corrections

Use zero horizontal resultant rather than treating tan 90° as a number. The boat arrives about 40.36 minutes before the walker.
