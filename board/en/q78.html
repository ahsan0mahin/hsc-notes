Part a · 1 mark

Define a vector field.

Hint Try it first

What must be given at each point?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It assigns a vector, with magnitude and direction, to each point of a region.

Part b · 2 marks

Is a reciprocal vector collinear with its original?

Hint Try it first

Compare directions rather than magnitudes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Yes. The reciprocal is a positive scalar multiple of the nonzero original, so their lines are parallel.

Part c · 3 marks

Calculate x.

Hint Try it first

Use the perpendicularity of V and B to set up an equation in x.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

12−2x=012-2x=0

Their dot product is 6+6−2x=0; hence x=6.

Part d · 4 marks

Verify that the shortest-time route exceeds the river width.

Hint Try it first

In the minimum-time case the ground track includes a nonzero downstream displacement.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

S/D=66/7>1S/D=\sqrt{66}/7>1

The speeds are 7 and √17. On the shortest path the crossing component is √(49−17). On the fastest crossing it is 7, but the ground speed is √66. The path-to-width ratio √66/7 exceeds one.
