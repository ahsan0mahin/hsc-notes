Question and stimulus

A boat starts at A at 16 km/h relative to water on a 5 km wide river flowing at 8 km/h. A person waits 25 min at B directly opposite.

  1. (a)

    What are Cartesian unit vectors?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why do all points on a rigid fan have the same angular velocity about its axis?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the resultant boat speed.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    How should the boatman steer directly to B, and will he meet the person?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

A is on the near bank and B on the opposite bank. The current arrow points downstream. Distinguish aiming directly across from landing directly opposite.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What are Cartesian unit vectors?

Hint Try it first

Look along the positive coordinate axes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They are three mutually perpendicular vectors of unit magnitude pointing along positive x, y and z.

Part b · 2 marks

Why do all points on a rigid fan have the same angular velocity about its axis?

Hint Try it first

Distinguish angular and linear displacement.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

In a given time every point turns through the same angle as the rigid fan. Angular speed is therefore common although tangential speed ωr varies with radius.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the resultant boat speed.

Hint Try it first

For the initial square crossing add current and boat velocities at right angles.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=162+82,tan⁡θ=16/8R=\sqrt{16^2+8^2},\quad\tan\theta=16/8

Assuming an initial heading squarely across, ground speed is √(16²+8²)≈17.9 km/h at 63.4° to downstream.

Part d · 4 marks

How should the boatman steer directly to B, and will he meet the person?

Hint Try it first

Landing directly opposite imposes zero drift; use the remaining component to test the 25 min limit.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡α=−8/16,t=516sin⁡120∘ h\cos\alpha=-8/16,\quad t=\frac{5}{16\sin120^\circ}\,\mathrm h

Steer at arccos(−8/16)=120° to the current. Crossing time=60×5/(16 sin120°)≈21.65 min, within the waiting period.
