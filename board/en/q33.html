Question and stimulus

A vehicle travels along riverside road AB (1.2 km) at 15 m/s and stops for 40 s at B. C is directly opposite B, 0.5 km away. Boat speed is 4 m/s and current speed is 2 m/s.

  1. (a)

    State the triangle law of vector addition.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why is a trolley handle long?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    What is the minimum crossing time?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    If the boat starts from C when the vehicle is at A, can the passengers board it at B?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

AB is the road along one bank. B and C lie across the 0.5 km width from each other. The drawing describes two journeys with separate times.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

State the triangle law of vector addition.

Hint Try it first

Arrange two arrows head to tail.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

If two sides taken in order represent the addends, the arrow from the first tail to the last head represents their sum.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is a trolley handle long?

Hint Try it first

At a fixed hand height compare the angle to the ground.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A longer handle can make a smaller angle to the horizontal. The same pull then has a greater forward component Fcos⁡θF\cos\theta.

Part c · 3 marks

What is the minimum crossing time?

Hint Try it first

What heading gives the greatest possible component across BC?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tmin⁡=500/4=125 st_{\min}=500/4=125\,\mathrm s

Aim perpendicular to the bank. The maximum crossing component is 4 m/s, so the minimum time is 500/4=125 s.

Part d · 4 marks

If the boat starts from C when the vehicle is at A, can the passengers board it at B?

Hint Try it first

Add the stop time to the vehicle travel time. Compare with the time to reach B rather than just any point on the bank.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tboat=50016−4>120015+40t_{\rm boat}=\frac{500}{\sqrt{16-4}}>\frac{1200}{15}+40

The vehicle leaves after 1200/15+40=120 s. To land directly at B takes 500/√12≈144.3 s; even the minimum crossing time exceeds the deadline.

Source notes and corrections

The departure time includes the 40 s stop. Compare it with the time to reach B itself, not merely the earliest time to reach the bank.
