Question and stimulus

A solid cylinder rolls down an incline. Calipers give a main-scale reading of 4.2 cm and Vernier coincidence 8, least count 0.01 cm. Its angular velocity is (2,−3,1) rad/s and radius vector (1,−2,2) m.

  1. (a)

    What is pitch?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Does vector multiplication commute? Explain.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the diameter and relative error in volume, taking the height of 10 cm as exact.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Find the linear velocity and show it is perpendicular to the radius vector.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is pitch?

Hint Try it first

Follow one complete turn of a screw.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Pitch is the axial advance of the screw in one complete revolution.

Part b · 2 marks

Does vector multiplication commute? Explain.

Hint Try it first

Reverse the order of the two arrows.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The magnitude remains AB sin θ but the right-hand direction reverses. Thus A×B=−B×A, unequal unless the product is zero.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the diameter and relative error in volume, taking the height of 10 cm as exact.

Hint Try it first

Combine the two instrument readings. With exact height, how does volume depend on diameter?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

D=4.2+8(0.01),ΔVV≈2ΔDDD=4.2+8(0.01),\quad\frac{\Delta V}{V}\approx2\frac{\Delta D}{D}

Diameter=4.2+8×0.01=4.28 cm. Since volume is proportional to diameter squared, relative uncertainty ≈2×0.01/4.28≈0.00467 (0.47%).

Part d · 4 marks

Find the linear velocity and show it is perpendicular to the radius vector.

Hint Try it first

Calculate ω×r in that order, then dot the result with r.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

v⃗=ω⃗×r⃗=(−4,−3,−1),v⃗⋅r⃗=0\vec v=\vec\omega\times\vec r=(-4,-3,-1),\quad\vec v\cdot\vec r=0

The cross product ω×r=(−4,−3,−1) m/s. Its dot product with r is −4+6−2=0.

Source notes and corrections

With exact height and diameter uncertainty 0.01 cm, first-order relative volume uncertainty is approximately 0.4673%.
