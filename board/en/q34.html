Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the gradient of a scalar field.

Hint Try it first

Compare rates of change in different directions.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Its components are the three spatial partial derivatives. It points in the direction of steepest increase; its magnitude is that maximum rate.

Part b · 2 marks

Compare the units and physical meanings of work and torque.

Hint Try it first

Compare a dot product with a cross product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Both have dimensions force times length. Work is a scalar in joules; torque is an axial vector in newton metres.

Part c · 3 marks

Find a unit vector parallel to PQ.

Hint Try it first

Subtract P from Q and normalize the resulting displacement.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u^PQ=−4i^+3j^−2k^29\hat u_{PQ}=\frac{-4\hat i+3\hat j-2\hat k}{\sqrt{29}}

Q−P=(−4,3,−2); divide by √29 to obtain the required unit vector.

Part d · 4 marks

Is OPQ a right triangle?

Hint Try it first

Check all three squared lengths, including the side that does not start at O.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

OP2+OQ2=23≠29OP^2+OQ^2=23\ne29

OP²=14, OQ²=9, PQ²=29. The sum 14+9 does not equal 29.

Source notes and corrections

A gradient gives both the direction and the maximum rate of increase. For the triangle the squared side lengths are 14, 9 and 29.
