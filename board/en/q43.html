Question and stimulus

Rahmat’s boat moves at 3 km/h in a 4 km/h current across a river 1.5 km wide. Aiming along AB leads to C. The diagram marks AC and AD at 30° to the bank on opposite sides.

  1. (a)

    Define an elastic collision.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Distinguish mass from moment of inertia.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the distance travelled along AC.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    If he aims along AD, can he reach B directly opposite?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

AB is perpendicular to the banks. AD slopes upstream and AC downstream, each marked at 30° to the bank. The printed angle and speed data do not all agree.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define an elastic collision.

Hint Try it first

Which two quantities are conserved?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Total momentum and total kinetic energy of the isolated system are both conserved.

Part b · 2 marks

Distinguish mass from moment of inertia.

Hint Try it first

Which quantity depends on the rotation axis?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Mass measures inertia in translation. Moment of inertia measures rotational inertia and depends on how mass is distributed relative to the axis: I=∑mr2I=\sum mr^2.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the distance travelled along AC.

Hint Try it first

Compute AC once using the given angle and once using the speeds. Do the source data agree?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

AC=1.52+(4⋅0.5)2AC=\sqrt{1.5^2+(4\cdot0.5)^2}

From the speeds, crossing time is 1.5/3=0.5 h and drift is 2 km, so AC=2.5 km. From the marked angle AC=1.5/sin30°=3 km. The data disagree.

Part d · 4 marks

If he aims along AD, can he reach B directly opposite?

Hint Try it first

For AD the upstream component must exactly cancel the river current.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Rx=4+3cos⁡150∘≠0R_x=4+3\cos150^\circ\ne0

Along AD the net downstream speed is 4+3 cos150°≈1.40 km/h, not zero. He cannot land at B.

Source notes and corrections

The 30° marking predicts a different length from the given velocities. The discrepancy is in the source data, not simply rounding.
