Part a · 1 mark

Define torque.

Hint Try it first

What distance matters about a pivot?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Torque is the moment of force about a point: τ⃗=r⃗×F⃗\vec\tau=\vec r\times\vec F.

Part b · 2 marks

What does an angular momentum of 2400 kg m²/s mean?

Hint Try it first

Relate angular momentum to moment of inertia and angular speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

About the stated axis the rotational momentum has magnitude 2400 kg m²/s; for fixed-axis rigid rotation Iω=2400. This does not by itself determine torque.

Part c · 3 marks

Find m.

Hint Try it first

Take the dot product using the unknown z component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

8−4m=08-4m=0

P·Q=8−4m=0, hence m=2.

Part d · 4 marks

Does a minimum-time crossing cover more distance than the width? Verify numerically.

Hint Try it first

Convert two minutes to seconds if using m/s. Distinguish minimum-time heading from minimum-path heading.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

D=12020 m,S=D56/38(v in m/s)D=120\sqrt{20}\,\mathrm m,\quad S=D\sqrt{56/38}\quad (v\text{ in m/s})

The speeds are √38 and √18. The direct crossing speed is √20; the minimum-time path-to-width ratio is √56/√38≈1.214. The route is longer.

Source notes and corrections

Angular momentum has units kg m²/s, not kg m²/s². If the speed components are in m/s, the two-minute direct crossing gives a width of about 536.66 m.
