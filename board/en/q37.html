Part a · 1 mark

What are equal vectors?

Hint Try it first

Check direction as well as length.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Vectors of the same kind are equal when their magnitudes and directions agree.

Part b · 2 marks

Can a vector differential operator turn a scalar into a vector?

Hint Try it first

Consider the spatial derivatives.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Yes. The gradient of a scalar field combines its partial derivatives along each axis into a vector pointing towards the steepest increase.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the length of PQ.

Hint Try it first

Compute the change in all three coordinates before taking the length.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∣PQ→∣=02+(−8)2+(−6)2|\overrightarrow{PQ}|=\sqrt{0^2+(-8)^2+(-6)^2}

PQ=(0,−8,−6); its length is √100=10.

Part d · 4 marks

Are the position vectors OP and OQ perpendicular?

Hint Try it first

Keep both position vectors based at O when taking their dot product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(2,4,2)⋅(2,−4,−4)=−20(2,4,2)\cdot(2,-4,-4)=-20

OP·OQ=4−16−8=−20. A nonzero dot product means the vectors are not perpendicular.
