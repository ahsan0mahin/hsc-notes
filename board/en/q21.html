Question and stimulus

A bus travels at 4 m/s along the bank of a 1 km wide river. A person starting directly opposite it steers a boat at 6 m/s at 70° to the 2 m/s current to catch it.

  1. (a)

    Define the position (radius) vector.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Can the magnitude of A×B equal A·B?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the boat’s resultant speed.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Can the person catch the bus? Justify.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Can the magnitude of A×B equal A·B?

Hint Try it first

Compare sine with cosine.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Yes. For nonzero vectors making 45°, AB sin45° and AB cos45° are equal.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the boat’s resultant speed.

Hint Try it first

Resolve the boat’s speed along and across the current, then add the current to only one component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=(2+6cos⁡70∘)2+(6sin⁡70∘)2R=\sqrt{(2+6\cos70^\circ)^2+(6\sin70^\circ)^2}

The components are (2+6 cos70°, 6 sin70°) m/s; their resultant is about 6.94 m/s.

Part d · 4 marks

Can the person catch the bus? Justify.

Hint Try it first

Compare how far the bus and the boat move downstream during the crossing.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t=10006sin⁡70∘,xboat−xbus=(2+6cos⁡70∘−4)tt=\frac{1000}{6\sin70^\circ},\quad x_{\rm boat}-x_{\rm bus}=(2+6\cos70^\circ-4)t

Crossing takes about 177.36 s. The boat lands roughly 719 m downstream while the bus travels roughly 709 m. If the bus continues along that bank, the person can wait for it just ahead.

Source notes and corrections

The angle between nonzero vectors lies in [0°,180°]; 225° is not another solution to (b). Catching the bus assumes it continues along the bank towards the boat’s landing point.
