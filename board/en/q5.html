Part a · 1 mark

Define curl.

Hint Try it first

Imagine a tiny paddle wheel in the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Curl, ∇×V⃗\nabla\times\vec V, measures local circulation per unit area. Its direction is the local axis of rotation given by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is divergence a scalar?

Hint Try it first

Look at how del combines with the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The dot product adds the three component derivatives into one signed number. It gives net outward flow, not a direction.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the scalar projection of OM on ON.

Hint Try it first

Find a unit vector along ON. Take its dot product with OM.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

OM→⋅ON→∣ON→∣=12−6−236+9+4\frac{\overrightarrow{OM}\cdot\overrightarrow{ON}}{|\overrightarrow{ON}|}=\frac{12-6-2}{\sqrt{36+9+4}}

OM·ON=12−6−2=4 and ON has length 7. The signed projection is 4/7.

Part d · 4 marks

Is OMN a right triangle? Verify.

Hint Try it first

Checking the angle at O alone is insufficient. Test the longest side using all three squared lengths.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

OM2+ON2=58≠MN2OM^2+ON^2=58\ne MN^2

OM²=9, ON²=49 and MN²=50. Since 9+49 is not 50, no right angle exists.

Source notes and corrections

Testing just the angle at O would not exclude a right angle at M or N. Comparing the longest side squared with the other two squares tests the whole triangle.
