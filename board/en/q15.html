Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is absolute humidity?

Hint Try it first

Identify the mass in a sample of moist air.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Absolute humidity is the mass of water vapour per unit volume of air.

Part b · 2 marks

Show that i^×j^=k^\hat i\times\hat j=\hat k.

Hint Try it first

Use the right hand in a right-handed coordinate system.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The magnitude is sin 90° = 1; curling from positive x towards positive y makes the thumb point along positive z.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the area of the parallelogram with adjacent sides A and B.

Hint Try it first

Two adjacent sides span a parallelogram. Which product combines base and perpendicular height?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Δ=∣A⃗×B⃗∣=196+49+49\Delta=|\vec A\times\vec B|=\sqrt{196+49+49}

A×B=(−14,−7,7), so the area is 767\sqrt6 square units.

Part d · 4 marks

Can the stated triangle be right-angled?

Hint Try it first

At a right angle the adjacent sides have zero dot product. Test all three pairs.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A⃗⋅B⃗=14,A⃗⋅C⃗=−4,B⃗⋅C⃗=−13\vec A\cdot\vec B=14,\quad\vec A\cdot\vec C=-4,\quad\vec B\cdot\vec C=-13

The three pairwise dot products are 14, −4 and −13. No pair is perpendicular. Moreover, the given components do not close a triangle; the premise is inconsistent.

Source notes and corrections

The side vectors as printed do not close. None of the three pairwise dot products vanishes either.
