Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is i^⋅i^\hat i\cdot\hat i not zero?

Hint Try it first

What is the angle of a vector with itself?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Both lengths are one and the angle is zero, so the dot product is one.

Part c · 3 marks

Project B onto the direction of A.

Hint Try it first

Make A into a unit vector before projecting B.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

compAB=−2+6+3538\mathrm{comp}_A B=\frac{-2+6+35}{\sqrt{38}}

A·B=39 and A has length √38. The scalar projection is 39/√38 ≈ 6.33.

Part d · 4 marks

Do all three vectors lie in one plane?

Hint Try it first

Use a normal to the plane of two vectors. Does the third have a component along that normal?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A⃗⋅(B⃗×C⃗)=−129\vec A\cdot(\vec B\times\vec C)=-129

The scalar triple product A·(B×C)=−129 is nonzero. Hence the vectors are not coplanar.
