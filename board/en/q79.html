Question and stimulus

From a car moving east at 40 km/h a driver sees a truck moving north at twice that speed. Take east as positive x and north as positive y.

  1. (a)

    Define curl.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why is the outer edge of a curved road raised?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    In what direction is the truck actually moving?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Find a vector perpendicular to the plane of the two true velocities.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define curl.

Hint Try it first

Imagine a tiny paddle wheel in the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Curl, ∇×V⃗\nabla\times\vec V, measures local circulation per unit area. Its direction is the local axis of rotation given by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is the outer edge of a curved road raised?

Hint Try it first

Resolve the normal reaction on the vehicle.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Banking gives the normal reaction an inward horizontal component that supplies part or all of the centripetal force, reducing reliance on friction.

Part c · 3 marks

In what direction is the truck actually moving?

Hint Try it first

Reverse the relative-velocity subtraction to recover the truck’s true heading.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

v⃗T=40i^+80j^\vec v_T=40\hat i+80\hat j

Add the car velocity to the relative truck velocity: (40,80) km/h. The direction is 63.43° north of east.

Part d · 4 marks

Find a vector perpendicular to the plane of the two true velocities.

Hint Try it first

Find a normal to the plane spanned by the car and truck velocities in one ground frame.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

n^=v⃗C×v⃗T∣v⃗C×v⃗T∣=k^\hat n=\frac{\vec v_C\times\vec v_T}{|\vec v_C\times\vec v_T|}=\hat k

The car and truck velocities are in the xy-plane. Their cross product is (0,0,3200); the positive z unit vector is normal to the plane.

Source notes and corrections

Without the necessary inward force a vehicle would continue tangentially. The relative and ground velocities of the truck must not be interchanged.
