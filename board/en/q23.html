Part a · 1 mark

Define temporal periodicity.

Hint Try it first

Follow the state at one fixed position.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A quantity is periodic in time if it repeats after a fixed interval T: f(t+T)=f(t)f(t+T)=f(t).

Part b · 2 marks

Why is a position vector localized?

Hint Try it first

Change the tail without changing the arrow.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It begins at a specified origin. Moving its tail would no longer locate the same point relative to that origin.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the angle between PQ and PR.

Hint Try it first

Build both displacement vectors from the common vertex P.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡θ=(1,0,−1)⋅(−1,−2,2)2⋅3\cos\theta=\frac{(1,0,-1)\cdot(-1,-2,2)}{\sqrt2\cdot3}

PQ=(1,0,−1) and PR=(−1,−2,2). Their dot product is −3, so cos θ=−1/√2 and θ=135°.

Part d · 4 marks

Are the position vectors of the three points coplanar?

Hint Try it first

Use the triple product of OP, OQ and OR, not simply the fact that three endpoints define a triangle.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

r⃗P⋅(r⃗Q×r⃗R)=−9\vec r_P\cdot(\vec r_Q\times\vec r_R)=-9

Their scalar triple product is −9, not zero. The three position vectors from the origin are not coplanar.

Source notes and corrections

Three points always lie in a plane. The question instead tests three position vectors based at the common origin.
