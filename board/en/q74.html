Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What are Cartesian unit vectors?

Hint Try it first

Look along the positive coordinate axes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They are three mutually perpendicular vectors of unit magnitude pointing along positive x, y and z.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is a position vector localized?

Hint Try it first

Change the tail without changing the arrow.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It begins at a specified origin. Moving its tail would no longer locate the same point relative to that origin.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the angle between B and C.

Hint Try it first

Divide B·C by the product of their magnitudes and check the sign before taking arccos.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡θ=−163514\cos\theta=\frac{-16}{\sqrt{35}\sqrt{14}}

B·C=−16; cos θ=−16/√490. Thus θ≈136.3°.

Part d · 4 marks

Test the claim that the triangle is right-angled.

Hint Try it first

Check the printed vector-sum condition as well as the squared side lengths.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

14+14≠35,B⃗+C⃗=(3,−2,2)14+14\ne35,\quad\vec B+\vec C=(3,-2,2)

Squared lengths are 14,35,14. They fail the Pythagorean test. Moreover B+C=(3,−2,2), not the printed A; the claim is inconsistent.

Source notes and corrections

The printed assertion A=B+C conflicts with the printed components, and the stated side lengths do not satisfy the right-triangle condition.
