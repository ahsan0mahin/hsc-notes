Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Why can water stay in a bucket swinging in a vertical circle?

Hint Try it first

Consider the top of the path.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

There the required acceleration is downward. Gravity and any bucket force supply it; with sufficient speed, v2/r≥gv^2/r\ge g, the water maintains contact.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the curl of A.

Hint Try it first

Write the components in their original order and take the cyclic differences of partial derivatives.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∇×A⃗=(4x2y3−6xz3, 6yz3, −8xy3z−8x3y)\nabla\times\vec A=(4x^2y^3-6xz^3,\ 6yz^3,\ -8xy^3z-8x^3y)

Taking the cyclic differences of component derivatives gives (4x2y3−6xz3,6yz3,−8xy3z−8x3y)(4x^2y^3-6xz^3,6yz^3,-8xy^3z-8x^3y).

Part d · 4 marks

Use the given fields to convert a scalar field into a vector field and a vector field into a scalar field.

Hint Try it first

Start with φ for one transformation and A for the other. What should the output type be in each case?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∇ϕ=(3y2z+3yz2, 6xyz+3xz2+6yz3, 3xy2+6xyz+9y2z2)\nabla\phi=(3y^2z+3yz^2,\ 6xyz+3xz^2+6yz^3,\ 3xy^2+6xyz+9y^2z^2)

Taking the gradient of φ gives the vector field shown in the equation. Taking the divergence of A gives the scalar field 12x2y2−12x2y2z−18xyz212x^2y^2-12x^2y^2z-18xyz^2. The two operations change the type of the output in opposite ways.

Source notes and corrections

In an inertial frame gravity still acts at the top of the bucket’s path. The limiting condition for maintaining contact there is v²/r ≥ g.
