Question and stimulus

Shanto’s college is directly across a 1 km wide river. Five minutes before class he aims his boat at 120° to the current, moving at 12 km/h relative to the water.

  1. (a)

    What is a spring constant?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why is walking on sand difficult?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Determine the current speed for direct arrival.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Can Shanto reach class on time?

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is a spring constant?

Hint Try it first

Compare extension with restoring force.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Within the linear elastic range, F=−kxF=-kx; k is the restoring force magnitude per unit extension.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is walking on sand difficult?

Hint Try it first

Resolve the ground reaction.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The foot pushes backwards. Loose sand gives way, reducing the forward frictional reaction and making each step less effective.

Part c · 3 marks

Determine the current speed for direct arrival.

Hint Try it first

A direct landing requires the horizontal component of the ground velocity to vanish.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

c=−12cos⁡120∘c=-12\cos120^\circ

To eliminate drift, current speed must be −12 cos120°=6 km/h.

Part d · 4 marks

Can Shanto reach class on time?

Hint Try it first

The current cannot increase the perpendicular component. Convert the resulting time to minutes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t=6012sin⁡120∘ mint=\frac{60}{12\sin120^\circ}\,\mathrm{min}

Crossing takes 60/(12 sin120°)≈5.77 min. He misses the start.

Source notes and corrections

Finding the current speed assumes that the resultant is directed exactly towards the college. The definition of a spring constant applies within the linear elastic range.
