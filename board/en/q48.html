Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is the centre of gravity?

Hint Try it first

Replace distributed weights by one resultant.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the point through which the resultant weight acts.

Part b · 2 marks

Why can a sphere falling through liquid acquire constant speed?

Hint Try it first

Compare weight, buoyancy and drag.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

As speed increases drag rises. When buoyancy plus drag balances weight, acceleration becomes zero and the sphere continues at terminal speed.

Part c · 3 marks

Find curl A at (1,−1,1).

Hint Try it first

Write the three cyclic differences of derivatives; substitute the point only afterwards.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∇×A⃗=(5xz2+3x2y,4xy2−5yz2,−14xyz)\nabla\times\vec A=(5xz^2+3x^2y,4xy^2-5yz^2,-14xyz)

Take the cyclic differences of component derivatives before substituting. The three components at the point are (2,9,14).

Source notes and corrections

φ and A are fields, not differential operators. A centre of gravity can lie outside the material of a body, as for a ring.
