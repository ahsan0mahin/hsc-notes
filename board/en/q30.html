Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define coplanar vectors.

Hint Try it first

Think of translating free arrows to a common origin.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Their directions can all lie in one plane; the scalar triple product of any three is zero.

Part b · 2 marks

Does divergence help determine a change in a fluid element’s volume?

Hint Try it first

Compare neighbouring flow velocities.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For a small material element of volume V, the fractional rate of expansion is (1/V)dV/dt=div u, where u is the velocity field.

Part c · 3 marks

Find m.

Hint Try it first

Take the dot product of P and Q and use the angular condition.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

8−4m=08-4m=0

P·Q=8−4m=0, hence m=2.

Part d · 4 marks

Does a minimum-time crossing cover more distance than the width? Verify numerically.

Hint Try it first

Compare the ratios of ground speed to perpendicular speed for the two crossings.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

SD=38+1838\frac{S}{D}=\frac{\sqrt{38+18}}{\sqrt{38}}

The speeds are √38 and √18. The direct crossing speed is √20; the minimum-time path-to-width ratio is √56/√38≈1.214. The route is longer.

Source notes and corrections
