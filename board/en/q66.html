Question and stimulus

Jamal lives at A and works at D. B is directly opposite A, and BD is 150 m downstream. He sets out at 10 m/s at 60° to upstream; the river flows at 6 m/s. His deadline is 5 min.

  1. (a)

    What is oscillatory motion?

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Describe the acceleration–displacement graph for simple harmonic motion.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    How far does Jamal’s boat travel across the river?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Does he reach his office within five minutes?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

A is Jamal’s home. B is directly opposite A and D is his office, 150 m downstream of B. The horizontal component determines the time needed to cover BD.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is oscillatory motion?

Hint Try it first

Watch the motion relative to equilibrium.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is repeated to-and-fro motion about an equilibrium position.

Part b · 2 marks

Describe the acceleration–displacement graph for simple harmonic motion.

Hint Try it first

Use the restoring acceleration.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

a=−ω2xa=-\omega^2x: the graph is a straight line through the origin with slope −ω². Acceleration always points towards equilibrium.

Part c · 3 marks

How far does Jamal’s boat travel across the river?

Hint Try it first

BD is the downstream drift, not the width. Use the along-bank component to obtain the time.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

vx=6+10cos⁡120∘,vy=10sin⁡120∘v_x=6+10\cos120^\circ,\quad v_y=10\sin120^\circ

Net downstream speed is 6−10 cos60°=1 m/s; across speed is 10 sin60°. Time=150/1=150 s, so the path is 150√76≈1308 m.

Part d · 4 marks

Does he reach his office within five minutes?

Hint Try it first

Compare the time corresponding to that drift with five minutes in the same units.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t=150/vx=150 st=150/v_x=150\,\mathrm s

150 s=2.5 min, less than the five-minute allowance.

Source notes and corrections

The downstream component is 1 m/s, the across component 5√3 m/s. The resultant is inclined about 6.59° from the perpendicular to the bank.
