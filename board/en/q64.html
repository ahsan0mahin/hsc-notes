Reading the diagram

OP and OR are adjacent sides; OQ is the diagonal. The marked angles α and β separate the diagonal from those two sides.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define degrees of freedom.

Hint Try it first

Count independent coordinates, not particles.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The number of independent coordinates required to specify a system’s configuration is its number of degrees of freedom.

Part b · 2 marks

At equal temperature do different one-mole ideal gas samples have the same mean translational kinetic energy?

Hint Try it first

Separate particle mass from temperature.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Yes. Each molecule has mean translational energy 3kT/2; one mole has 3RT/2, independent of molecular species.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the scalar projection of B onto A.

Hint Try it first

A is the reference direction. Normalize it before taking its dot product with B.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

compAB=12−6−23\mathrm{comp}_A B=\frac{12-6-2}{3}

A·B=12−6−2=4 and A has length 3. The projection is 4/3.

Part d · 4 marks

Can A and B be adjacent sides of a parallelogram?

Hint Try it first

Adjacent sides need not be perpendicular, but they must span a nonzero area.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A⃗×B⃗=(1,−10,−18)≠0⃗\vec A\times\vec B=(1,-10,-18)\ne\vec0

Yes: both are nonzero and neither is a scalar multiple of the other. Their cross product is nonzero, so they enclose a nonzero area.

Source notes and corrections

Per molecule the mean translational energy is 3kT/2. The corresponding total for one mole is 3RT/2, not the mean energy of a single molecule.
