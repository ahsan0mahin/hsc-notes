Question and stimulus

Dipu steers along OB at 7.2 km/h at 120° to the current and lands directly at his college at A across a 2.5 km wide river.

  1. (a)

    Define curl.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Can two unequal vectors of the same kind have zero resultant?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the current speed.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Will he reach class if he starts 25 min before it begins?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

A is the college directly across from O. The boat points upstream along OB at 120° to the current; its actual path is OA.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define curl.

Hint Try it first

Imagine a tiny paddle wheel in the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Curl, ∇×V⃗\nabla\times\vec V, measures local circulation per unit area. Its direction is the local axis of rotation given by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

Can two unequal vectors of the same kind have zero resultant?

Hint Try it first

Place them head to tail.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

No. Complete cancellation requires equal magnitudes and opposite directions.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the current speed.

Hint Try it first

Direct arrival at the college fixes the net along-bank velocity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

c=−7.2cos⁡120∘c=-7.2\cos120^\circ

Zero drift requires current speed −7.2 cos120°=3.6 km/h.

Part d · 4 marks

Will he reach class if he starts 25 min before it begins?

Hint Try it first

Use the perpendicular component and express the arrival time in minutes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t=2.57.2sin⁡120∘⋅60 mint=\frac{2.5}{7.2\sin120^\circ}\cdot60\,\mathrm{min}

Time=60×2.5/(7.2 sin120°)≈24.06 min. Yes, he arrives before class.

Source notes and corrections

Opposite vectors can cancel only if their magnitudes agree. The unrounded crossing time is approximately 24.056 min.
