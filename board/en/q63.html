Question and stimulus

Boat speeds downstream and upstream are 18 and 8 km/h. The river is 3 km wide. One boat heads for the opposite point. A second boat of equal speed is described as heading at 100° to the upstream direction.

  1. (a)

    Define torque.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Explain why gravity is a conservative force.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Which direction gives a direct landing?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Calculate the downstream displacement for the minimum-time crossing.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define torque.

Hint Try it first

What distance matters about a pivot?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Torque is the moment of force about a point: τ⃗=r⃗×F⃗\vec\tau=\vec r\times\vec F.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain why gravity is a conservative force.

Hint Try it first

Compare the work along two routes joining the same heights.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The work equals the loss of gravitational potential energy and depends only on the endpoints. Around a closed path the net work by gravity is zero.

Part c · 3 marks

Which direction gives a direct landing?

Hint Try it first

Add and subtract the measured velocities, then find the heading that cancels the current.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u=(18+8)/2,c=(18−8)/2u=(18+8)/2,\quad c=(18-8)/2

Boat speed is (18+8)/2=13 km/h; current speed is 5 km/h. Hence α=arccos(−5/13)≈112.62° to downstream.

Part d · 4 marks

Calculate the downstream displacement for the minimum-time crossing.

Hint Try it first

For minimum time use the entire boat speed across the width; calculate downstream drift over that interval.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

x=5(3/13) kmx=5(3/13)\,\mathrm{km}

Minimum time requires aiming perpendicular to the bank, regardless of the separately stated 100° heading. Time=3/13 h and drift=5×3/13≈1.15 km.

Source notes and corrections

The still-water speed is 13 km/h. The heading for a direct landing is measured from downstream; a fixed heading 100° to upstream is not the minimum-time heading.
