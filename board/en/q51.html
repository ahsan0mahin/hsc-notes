Question and stimulus

A delivery drone flies from warehouse A(2,3,1) to customer B(8,7,5), via rooftop checkpoint C(5,5,3).

  1. (a)

    Define vector multiplication (the cross product).

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    How are boat and current velocities represented as vectors?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the angle between AC and CB.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    At a constant 10 m/s is travelling via C faster than flying directly from A to B?

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define vector multiplication (the cross product).

Hint Try it first

Think about an oriented area.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A⃗×B⃗\vec A\times\vec B has magnitude ABsin⁡θAB\sin\theta and points normal to the two vectors by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

How are boat and current velocities represented as vectors?

Hint Try it first

Name the frame for each velocity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Draw the boat’s speed and heading relative to water and the water velocity relative to the bank. Their vector sum gives boat velocity relative to the bank.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the angle between AC and CB.

Hint Try it first

Are the displacements from A to C and from C to B proportional?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

AC→=CB→=(3,2,2)\overrightarrow{AC}=\overrightarrow{CB}=(3,2,2)

AC=CB=(3,2,2). The vectors have the same direction: θ=0°.

Part d · 4 marks

At a constant 10 m/s is travelling via C faster than flying directly from A to B?

Hint Try it first

Compare the sum of the segment lengths with the direct distance before dividing by speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tACB=21710=tABt_{ACB}=\frac{2\sqrt{17}}{10}=t_{AB}

C is the midpoint of AB. Both routes cover 2√17 m in 2√17/10 s; neither is faster.

Source notes and corrections

C is exactly the midpoint of AB. With these coordinates the alleged detour does not actually bend around the building.
