Question and stimulus

Sujan heads along AB at 6 m/s. Kuber heads along AC at 5.5 m/s, making 120° with the current of 1.2 m/s along CB. AB spans the 400 m river.

  1. (a)

    Define the position (radius) vector.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why is a trolley handle long?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find Kuber’s actual speed across the river relative to land.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Who reaches the opposite bank first?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

B is directly opposite A; C is upstream of B. Sujan aims along AB, Kuber along AC. The current runs from C towards B, at 120° to AC.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is a trolley handle long?

Hint Try it first

At a fixed hand height compare the angle to the ground.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A longer handle can make a smaller angle to the horizontal. The same pull then has a greater forward component Fcos⁡θF\cos\theta.

Part c · 3 marks

Find Kuber’s actual speed across the river relative to land.

Hint Try it first

The angle between the boat’s heading and the current is obtuse. Check the sign of the cross term in the squared speed.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

R=5.52+1.22+2(5.5)(1.2)cos⁡120∘R=\sqrt{5.5^2+1.2^2+2(5.5)(1.2)\cos120^\circ}

Adding the velocities gives 5.52+1.22+2(5.5)(1.2)cos⁡120∘≈5.01\sqrt{5.5^2+1.2^2+2(5.5)(1.2)\cos120^\circ}\approx5.01 m/s.

Part d · 4 marks

Who reaches the opposite bank first?

Hint Try it first

Both boats cross the same width; compare their perpendicular speed components.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

4006<4005.5sin⁡120∘\frac{400}{6}<\frac{400}{5.5\sin120^\circ}

Sujan takes 400/6≈66.7 s. Kuber takes 400/(5.5 sin120°)≈84 s. Sujan arrives first.
