Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is the dew point?

Hint Try it first

Cool an air sample without changing its vapour content.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the temperature at which the air becomes saturated and condensation can begin.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is a door handle near its outer edge?

Hint Try it first

Compare the moment arms.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For the same force the torque increases with perpendicular distance from the hinge, so less force is needed farther out.

Part c · 3 marks

At what speed does rain strike the front glass relative to the car?

Hint Try it first

Work in the car frame: subtract the entire car velocity vector from the rain velocity.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

v⃗rc=u⃗−v⃗=−18i^−12j^\vec v_{rc}=\vec u-\vec v=-18\hat i-12\hat j

Subtract the car velocity: relative velocity is (−18,−12), of magnitude 6√13≈21.63 in the given speed units.

Part d · 4 marks

Will the rear glass get wet? Analyse the geometry.

Hint Try it first

Compare the downward slope of the relative rain path with the slope of the rear window.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tan⁡−1(12/18)>30∘\tan^{-1}(12/18)>30^\circ

The drops move backwards at 33.69° below horizontal, steeper than the 30° rear slope. An exposed rear surface can therefore receive drops.

Source notes and corrections

The stem does not specify a speed unit. Front and rear glass have the same rain velocity relative to the car; incidence depends on the surface slope and shielding.
