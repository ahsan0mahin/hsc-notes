Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is turbulent flow?

Hint Try it first

Follow neighbouring fluid particles.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Velocity fluctuates irregularly in space and time, with eddies and mixing rather than smooth layers.

Part b · 2 marks

Does vector multiplication commute? Explain.

Hint Try it first

Reverse the order of the two arrows.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The magnitude remains AB sin θ but the right-hand direction reverses. Thus A×B=−B×A, unequal unless the product is zero.

Part c · 3 marks

Find ∇(1/r)\nabla(1/r).

Hint Try it first

Write 1/r as a power of x²+y²+z² before differentiating.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∇(1/r)=−r⃗/r3\nabla(1/r)=-\vec r/r^3

Differentiate (x2+y2+z2)−1/2(x^2+y^2+z^2)^{-1/2} componentwise to obtain −r⃗/r3-\vec r/r^3 for r≠0.

Part d · 4 marks

Find the curl of r⃗/r2\vec r/r^2 and classify the field.

Hint Try it first

Can the field be expressed as a gradient away from the origin?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

∇×(r⃗/r2)=∇×∇ln⁡r=0⃗(r>0)\nabla\times(\vec r/r^2)=\nabla\times\nabla\ln r=\vec0\quad(r>0)

The field is the gradient of ln r away from the origin; its curl is therefore zero and it is irrotational there.
