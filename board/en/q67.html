Question and stimulus

A river flows at 3 km/h. A boat moving at 5 km/h relative to water takes 6 min to land directly opposite. Another boat aims along AB at 6 km/h and reaches C.

  1. (a)

    Define unlike vectors.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why is j^×i^=−k^\hat j\times\hat i=-\hat k?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the river width.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Determine the distance AC.

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

AB shows the width; AC is the diagonal ground path, and BC is the drift. The boat’s heading and its actual direction need not coincide.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define unlike vectors.

Hint Try it first

Compare the senses of parallel arrows.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

They are vectors of the same kind directed in opposite senses along parallel lines.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is j^×i^=−k^\hat j\times\hat i=-\hat k?

Hint Try it first

Reverse the order of the usual cyclic product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Interchanging two factors reverses the cross product. Since i×j=k, j×i=−k.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the river width.

Hint Try it first

For the first direct crossing the upstream component cancels the current. Use the remaining component to recover width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

AB=52−32(6/60)AB=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}(6/60)

The direct crossing speed is √(25−9)=4 km/h; width=4×6/60=0.4 km.

Part d · 4 marks

Determine the distance AC.

Hint Try it first

On the second trip AB is the heading, not the ground track. Add drift geometrically to AB.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

AC=0.42+(3⋅0.4/6)2AC=\sqrt{0.4^2+(3\cdot0.4/6)^2}

Time=0.4/6 h and drift=0.2 km. AC=√(0.4²+0.2²)≈0.447 km.

Source notes and corrections

Clockwise and counterclockwise depend on the viewing side. In right-handed Cartesian axes the order of the cross product fixes its sign unambiguously.
