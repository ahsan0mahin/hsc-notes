Part a · 1 mark

What is a null vector?

Hint Try it first

Imagine coincident start and end points.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Its magnitude is zero and it has no uniquely defined direction.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain the physical significance of divergence.

Hint Try it first

Compare flow into and out of a small volume.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For a velocity field positive divergence indicates net local outflow, negative divergence net inflow, and zero divergence no net volume flux per unit volume.

Part d · 4 marks

Can the scalar product equal the magnitude of the cross product? Find the possible values of a.

Hint Try it first

Take the magnitude of the cross product before equating it to the scalar product.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

8−a=22 ∣a+1∣8-a=2\sqrt2\,|a+1|

Solve 8−a=22 ∣a+1∣8-a=2\sqrt2\,\lvert a+1\rvert. The two branches give a=(8−22)/(1+22)a=(8-2\sqrt2)/(1+2\sqrt2) or a=(8+22)/(1−22)a=(8+2\sqrt2)/(1-2\sqrt2). Both satisfy the original equation.
