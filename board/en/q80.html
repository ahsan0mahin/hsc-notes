Question and stimulus

Riyan swims at 4 km/h from the south bank towards a point directly north. The current flows west to east at 2 km/h.

  1. (a)

    Define a unit vector.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Explain the physical significance of divergence.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    In which direction should Riyan swim?

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Are the shortest-path and minimum-time crossing times equal? Verify.

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define a unit vector.

Hint Try it first

Think about direction separately from length.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A unit vector has magnitude one. Along a nonzero vector A it is A⃗/∣A⃗∣\vec A/|\vec A|.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain the physical significance of divergence.

Hint Try it first

Compare flow into and out of a small volume.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For a velocity field positive divergence indicates net local outflow, negative divergence net inflow, and zero divergence no net volume flux per unit volume.

Part c · 3 marks

In which direction should Riyan swim?

Hint Try it first

Treat north and east as separate axes. Which component of swimming velocity should point west?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

4sin⁡θ=24\sin\theta=2

To cancel eastward drift he needs a 2 km/h westward component. Thus aim arcsin(2/4)=30° west of north.

Part d · 4 marks

Are the shortest-path and minimum-time crossing times equal? Verify.

Hint Try it first

For a symbolic width d write times for maximum across-bank speed and for zero drift separately.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tdirect=d/12>d/4=tmin⁡t_{\rm direct}=d/\sqrt{12}>d/4=t_{\min}

For width d the direct-landing time is d/√12. The minimum time is d/4, achieved by heading due north and drifting east. The times differ.

Source notes and corrections

The specified heading is unique only if the swimmer must land directly opposite the start. Simply reaching the other bank does not fix one heading.
