Question and stimulus

Three forces act at O on a particle initially at rest: 11 N towards +x, 22 N at 60° above −x, and 33 N at 30° to the left of −y.

  1. (a)

    Define an elastic collision.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Does vector multiplication commute? Explain.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find the resultant force component along OX.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    In which direction will the particle start moving?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

Use O as the origin: F₁ lies on +x, F₂ in quadrant II, F₃ in quadrant III. The angles of the last two are measured from different axes.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define an elastic collision.

Hint Try it first

Which two quantities are conserved?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Total momentum and total kinetic energy of the isolated system are both conserved.

Part b · 2 marks

Does vector multiplication commute? Explain.

Hint Try it first

Reverse the order of the two arrows.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The magnitude remains AB sin θ but the right-hand direction reverses. Thus A×B=−B×A, unequal unless the product is zero.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the resultant force component along OX.

Hint Try it first

Take east as positive. Express all directions relative to +x before summing horizontal components.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Fx=11+22cos⁡120∘+33cos⁡240∘=−16.5 NF_x=11+22\cos120^\circ+33\cos240^\circ=-16.5\,\mathrm N

The x component is 11−22 cos60°−33 sin30°=−16.5 N, i.e. 16.5 N west.

Part d · 4 marks

In which direction will the particle start moving?

Hint Try it first

Find the vertical component as well. The signs of both components determine the correct quadrant.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Fy=(22−33)sin⁡60∘,θ=180∘+tan⁡−1∣Fy/Fx∣F_y=(22-33)\sin60^\circ,\quad\theta=180^\circ+\tan^{-1}|F_y/F_x|

The y component is 22 sin60°−33 cos30°≈−9.53 N. Both components are negative; the angle is 30° south of west, or 210° from +x.

Source notes and corrections

Use Fy/Fx for the slope and both component signs for the quadrant. The x component is −16.5 N and its absolute magnitude is 16.5 N.
