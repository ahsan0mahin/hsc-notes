Reading the diagram

The two position vectors start at O. The short arrow PQ joins their tips. At vertex P use PO, not OP, when measuring the angle.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define the position (radius) vector.

Hint Try it first

Choose an origin first.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the directed displacement from the chosen origin O to the point P, r⃗=OP→\vec r=\overrightarrow{OP}.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is a trolley handle long?

Hint Try it first

At a fixed hand height compare the angle to the ground.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A longer handle can make a smaller angle to the horizontal. The same pull then has a greater forward component Fcos⁡θF\cos\theta.

Part c · 3 marks

Find a unit vector parallel to PQ in the direction P to Q.

Hint Try it first

Which coordinate changes when you travel from P to Q? Normalize that displacement.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u^PQ=(2−1,3−3,4−4)∣(1,0,0)∣=i^\hat u_{PQ}=\frac{(2-1,3-3,4-4)}{|(1,0,0)|}=\hat i

Q−P=(1,0,0), already of unit length.

Part d · 4 marks

Are angles POQ and OPQ equal? Justify.

Hint Try it first

Identify the sides facing the angles at O and P. What would follow if those angles were equal?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡∠POQ=2726⋅29,cos⁡∠OPQ=−126\cos\angle POQ=\frac{27}{\sqrt{26\cdot29}},\quad\cos\angle OPQ=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{26}}

Equal angles would face equal sides. The side facing O has length PQ=1; the side facing P has length OQ=√29. The angles are unequal. Numerically, ∠POQ≈10.49° and ∠OPQ≈101.31°.

Source notes and corrections

Keeping the unrounded cosines gives angles of approximately 10.49° and 101.31° respectively.
