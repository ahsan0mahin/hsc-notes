A boat travels at 20 km/h downstream and 10 km/h upstream. The river is 2 km wide.

    3 marks

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

The double-ended line AB spans the 2 km width. The speed needed for a crossing time is the velocity component parallel to AB.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define curl.

Hint Try it first

Imagine a tiny paddle wheel in the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Curl, ∇×V⃗\nabla\times\vec V, measures local circulation per unit area. Its direction is the local axis of rotation given by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain why r⃗=5i^+3j^\vec r=5\hat i+3\hat j is a localized position vector.

Hint Try it first

Distinguish a position from a free displacement.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Its tail is at the chosen origin and its head is at (5,3). Moving its tail would no longer specify that position.

Part c · 3 marks

In which direction should the boat head from A to reach B directly opposite?

Hint Try it first

Separate the still-water speed from the current using the two measured speeds.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u=(20+10)/2,c=(20−10)/2,cos⁡α=−c/uu=(20+10)/2,\quad c=(20-10)/2,\quad\cos\alpha=-c/u

Still-water speed is 15 and current speed 5 km/h. Solve 15 cos α+5=0: α≈109.47° from downstream.

Part d · 4 marks

Would aiming straight across take more or less time?

Hint Try it first

Distinguish a direct ground track from an initial heading perpendicular to the bank.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t1=2152−52,t2=215(h)t_1=\frac{2}{\sqrt{15^2-5^2}},\quad t_2=\frac{2}{15}\quad(\mathrm h)

Direct landing takes 2/√200 h≈8.49 min. Aiming across takes 2/15 h=8 min and allows drift. It takes less time.

Source notes and corrections

Part (b) treats r as a position vector; listing components alone does not specify a fixed tail. The unrounded direct crossing time is about 8.485 min.
