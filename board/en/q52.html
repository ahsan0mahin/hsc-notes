Question and stimulus

On a 1.5 km wide river with current speed 9 m/s a boat heads at α to the current and travels directly across at a ground speed of 12 m/s.

    3 marks

    Would heading perpendicular to the bank take more or less time?

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define torque.

Hint Try it first

What distance matters about a pivot?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Torque is the moment of force about a point: τ⃗=r⃗×F⃗\vec\tau=\vec r\times\vec F.

Part b · 2 marks

In what sense is a conservative force path independent?

Hint Try it first

Compare two paths between the same endpoints.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is the work of the force that is path independent: W=U(initial)−U(final). Its integral around a closed path is zero.

Part c · 3 marks

Calculate α.

Hint Try it first

Draw the known perpendicular ground velocity and subtract the current to recover the boat’s heading.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u=92+122=15,cos⁡α=−9/15u=\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15,\quad\cos\alpha=-9/15

The boat’s own velocity is (−9,12), with length 15. Thus cos α=−9/15 and α≈126.87°.

Part d · 4 marks

Would heading perpendicular to the bank take more or less time?

Hint Try it first

Use the same width for both crossings but not the same perpendicular component.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

1500/12−1500/15=25 s1500/12-1500/15=25\,\mathrm s

Direct landing takes 1500/12=125 s. Heading perpendicular takes 1500/15=100 s: 25 s less.

Source notes and corrections

Aiming straight across and travelling straight across are different. In (b), it is the work done by a conservative force that is path independent.
