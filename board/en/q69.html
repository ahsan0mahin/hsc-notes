Question and stimulus

Anil crosses a 2 km wide river in still water in 5 min 20 s. With a 3 m/s current he aims directly towards his friend Dhruba standing opposite.

  1. (a)

    Define scalar projection.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Explain bird flight using vector addition.

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find Anil’s actual speed.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    How could he land at his friend’s position?

    4 marks

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define scalar projection.

Hint Try it first

Drop a perpendicular onto the reference line.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The signed component of A along B is A⃗⋅B⃗/∣B⃗∣=Acos⁡θ\vec A\cdot\vec B/|\vec B|=A\cos\theta.

Part b · 2 marks

Explain bird flight using vector addition.

Hint Try it first

Resolve aerodynamic forces parallel and perpendicular to the ground.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The forces from the wings add vectorially. Their upward resultant component can balance weight, while a forward component supplies thrust against drag.

Part c · 3 marks

Find Anil’s actual speed.

Hint Try it first

The still-water trial gives Anil’s own speed; add the new current as a perpendicular vector.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

u=2000/320,R=u2+32u=2000/320,\quad R=\sqrt{u^2+3^2}

Still-water speed is 2000/320=6.25 m/s. Combining it at right angles with the current gives √(6.25²+3²)≈6.93 m/s.

Part d · 4 marks

How could he land at his friend’s position?

Hint Try it first

Choose a swimming direction with an upstream component equal to the current.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

sin⁡θ=3/u\sin\theta=3/u

Aim upstream so the downstream component vanishes. The tilt upstream from straight across must satisfy 6.25 sin θ=3, giving θ≈28.69°.

Source notes and corrections

The total ground speed is not just the downstream component. A heading 28.69° upstream of the perpendicular is 118.69° from the current.
