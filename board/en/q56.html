VECTOR / COLLEGE PRACTICE

Adamjee Cantonment College, Dhaka

Original question 1 · Collection 56

Question and stimulus

Three students must reach B from O across a 2 km wide river within 25 min. Two boats aim straight across and land at C. The first student walks to B at 1.6 m/s; the second rows upstream from C to B. The third aims upstream along OA to land at B. Downstream and upstream speeds are 15 and 5 km/h.

  1. (a)

    Define displacement vector.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Can two unequal vectors of the same kind have zero resultant?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Calculate BC.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Can all three students reach class on time?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

OB runs directly across. Boats aiming along OB drift to C. A boat aiming upstream along OA can instead land at B.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define displacement vector.

Hint Try it first

Compare endpoints rather than the route between them.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is final position minus initial position: Δr⃗=r⃗f−r⃗i\Delta\vec r=\vec r_f-\vec r_i.

Part b · 2 marks

Can two unequal vectors of the same kind have zero resultant?

Hint Try it first

Place them head to tail.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

No. Complete cancellation requires equal magnitudes and opposite directions.

Part c · 3 marks

Calculate BC.

Hint Try it first

Infer both still-water and current speeds from the upstream and downstream values.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

BC=5(2/10) kmBC=5(2/10)\,\mathrm{km}

The boat and current speeds are 10 and 5 km/h. Crossing time is 2/10 h, so BC=1 km.

Part d · 4 marks

Can all three students reach class on time?

Hint Try it first

Separate the crossing time from each student’s extra walking or rowing time.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(t1,t2,t3)≈(22.42,24,13.86) min(t_1,t_2,t_3)\approx(22.42,24,13.86)\,\mathrm{min}

First: 12 min plus 1000/1.6 s≈22.42 min. Second: 12 min plus 1/5 h=24 min. Third: 2/√75 h≈13.86 min. All arrive within 25 min.

Source notes and corrections

Ignore time needed to turn the boats or to disembark. In (b), unequal means unequal magnitudes, not merely unequal vectors.
