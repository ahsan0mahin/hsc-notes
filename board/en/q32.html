Part a · 1 mark

What is a reciprocal vector?

Hint Try it first

Compare direction and the product of magnitudes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

For a nonzero vector A its reciprocal has the same direction and reciprocal magnitude: A⃗/∣A⃗∣2\vec A/|\vec A|^2.

Part b · 2 marks

Why does a lawn roller feel heavier when pushed?

Hint Try it first

Draw the vertical component of the push.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

A sloping push adds a downward component, increasing the normal reaction and rolling resistance without changing the roller’s actual weight.

Part c · 3 marks

Project A along B.

Hint Try it first

The reference direction is B; which length should divide the dot product?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

3+4+31+4+9=1014\frac{3+4+3}{\sqrt{1+4+9}}=\frac{10}{\sqrt{14}}

A·B=10 and the length of B is √14. The projection is 10/√14≈2.673.

Part d · 4 marks

Are A, B and C coplanar?

Hint Try it first

Compute B×C and test whether A has a component along it.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(3,2,1)⋅(−2,1,0)=−4(3,2,1)\cdot(-2,1,0)=-4

B×C=(−2,1,0); dotting this with A gives −4. Nonzero triple product rules out coplanarity.

Source notes and corrections
