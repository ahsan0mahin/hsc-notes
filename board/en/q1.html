Question and stimulus

In a boat race, Karim aims along OA and reaches B at a ground speed of 5.3 km/h. Rahim aims along OB and lands at R. The river is 2 km wide, the current is 2 km/h and both boats travel at 5 km/h relative to the water. Rain starts falling at 10 km/h.

    3 marks

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

O is the departure point. OA points upstream of the perpendicular OB; OR points downstream. The current runs to the right. Do not confuse the direction in which a boat is pointed with its actual track.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

What is divergence?

Hint Try it first

Consider the flux through the faces of a tiny closed box.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Divergence is the net outward flux per unit volume in the limit of a vanishing volume: ∇⋅V⃗\nabla\cdot\vec V.

Part b · 2 marks

Why is walking on sand difficult?

Hint Try it first

Resolve the ground reaction.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The foot pushes backwards. Loose sand gives way, reducing the forward frictional reaction and making each step less effective.

Part c · 3 marks

How long does Rahim take to cross?

Hint Try it first

Use the component along OB, not the length of OR. Divide the width by this component and convert hours to minutes.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t=dvsin⁡α=25sin⁡90∘ h=24 mint=\frac{d}{v\sin\alpha}=\frac{2}{5\sin90^\circ}\,\mathrm h=24\,\mathrm{min}

Only the across-river component covers the width: t=2/(5sin⁡90∘)=0.4t=2/(5\sin90^\circ)=0.4 h, or 24 min.

Part d · 4 marks

At what angle should Karim hold his umbrella? Justify your answer.

Hint Try it first

Draw the vertical rain velocity and subtract Karim’s horizontal ground velocity. Which angle is measured from vertical?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

tan⁡θ=vboatvrain=5.310\tan\theta=\frac{v_{\rm boat}}{v_{\rm rain}}=\frac{5.3}{10}

Assuming vertical rain, its velocity relative to Karim has a backward component of 5.3 and a downward component of 10 km/h. Tilt the umbrella forwards from the vertical by tan⁡−1(5.3/10)=27.92∘\tan^{-1}(5.3/10)=27.92^\circ.

Source notes and corrections

The boat speed is in km/h. Vertical rainfall is an assumption of the printed solution; the stem gives the rain speed but does not explicitly state its direction.
