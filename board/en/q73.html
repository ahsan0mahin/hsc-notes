Question and stimulus

A river 15 km wide flows at 5 km/h. Two boats travel at 10 km/h. The first heads at α to the current; the second heads at 90° to it.

  1. (a)

    Define displacement vector.

    1 mark
  2. (b)

    Why are not all collinear vectors equal?

    2 marks
  3. (c)

    Find α for the first boat to land directly opposite.

    3 marks
  4. (d)

    Which boat reaches the far bank first?

    4 marks

Reading the diagram

The second boat points across. For the first, the text measures α from the current but the sketch marks an angle from the perpendicular. State your reference line.

Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define displacement vector.

Hint Try it first

Compare endpoints rather than the route between them.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

It is final position minus initial position: Δr⃗=r⃗f−r⃗i\Delta\vec r=\vec r_f-\vec r_i.

Part b · 2 marks

Why are not all collinear vectors equal?

Hint Try it first

Test magnitude and direction separately.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Collinear vectors may differ in length or point in opposite directions. Equality requires both magnitude and direction to agree.

Part c · 3 marks

Find α for the first boat to land directly opposite.

Hint Try it first

Be clear whether α is measured from the bank or from the perpendicular before using cosine.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

cos⁡α=−5/10\cos\alpha=-5/10

10 cos α+5=0 gives 120° from downstream, or 30° upstream from the perpendicular.

Part d · 4 marks

Which boat reaches the far bank first?

Hint Try it first

Compare only the components normal to the banks for the same 15 km width.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

t1=15/(10sin⁡120∘),t2=15/10t_1=15/(10\sin120^\circ),\quad t_2=15/10

The first takes 15/(10 sin120°)≈1.73 h; the second takes 15/10=1.5 h and arrives first.

Source notes and corrections

The required heading is 120° to the current or 30° upstream of the perpendicular; these are different reference directions.
