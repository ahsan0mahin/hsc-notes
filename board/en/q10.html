Try it yourself

Part a · 1 mark

Define curl.

Hint Try it first

Imagine a tiny paddle wheel in the field.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

Curl, ∇×V⃗\nabla\times\vec V, measures local circulation per unit area. Its direction is the local axis of rotation given by the right-hand rule.

Part b · 2 marks

Why does the resultant of two equal vectors bisect their angle?

Hint Try it first

Draw the parallelogram and its diagonal.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

The parallelogram is a rhombus. Its diagonal separates two congruent triangles, so the angles between the resultant and the two addends are equal.

Part c · 3 marks

Find the scalar projection of A along B.

Hint Try it first

Which vector’s magnitude belongs in the denominator when B is the reference?

Full solution Diagram and explanation

compBA=2046\mathrm{comp}_B A=\frac{20}{\sqrt{46}}

A·B=20 and B has length √46, so the projection is 20/√46.

Part d · 4 marks

Can the two angles shown be equal for these vectors?

Hint Try it first

Check the signs of the two dot products before attempting to calculate either angle.

Full solution Diagram and explanation

(A⃗+B⃗)⋅(A⃗−B⃗)=∣A⃗∣2−∣B⃗∣2=−34(\vec A+\vec B)\cdot(\vec A-\vec B)=|\vec A|^2-|\vec B|^2=-34

A·B is positive, so the first angle is acute. (A+B)·(A−B)=12−46<0, so the second angle is obtuse. They cannot be equal.
